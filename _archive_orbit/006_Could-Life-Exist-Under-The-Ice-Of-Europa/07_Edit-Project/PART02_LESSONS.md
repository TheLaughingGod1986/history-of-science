## What shipped (quality bar)

## Locked preferences (user QA → Part 03+)

1. Match Part 01 + early Part 02 **CGI realism** — not cel/cartoon outlines.
2. **One** bottom-center soft glow; tiny bubbles **only when submerged**.
3. Above ice / vacuum: dry glow + ice reflection only — **no bubbles**.
4. Water whoosh/bubble bed **only** on `env: underwater` windows.
5. Travel poses on ice flyovers: look along flight path (not always at camera).
6. Exactly **one face** (one front visor) — reject clear second rear faceplate/eyes when spotted; keep regenerating stubborn plates when time allows.
7. Pupils always; single Orbit always.

## Inventory recipe

~8–10 unique Omni plates × ~8s for ~60–80s VO @ 1×. Never freeze-pad.
