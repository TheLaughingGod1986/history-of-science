# Explainer Styleboard Prompt v01

Tool: built-in image generation

Reference role: the supplied ElevenLabs screenshot was used only as aesthetic
inspiration. The surrounding interface, border, labels, mascot silhouette and
text were explicitly excluded.

## Prompt

Create one seamless 2-by-2 grid containing four distinct, coordinated 16:9
style frames for a premium space-science explainer. Each quadrant must be a
complete cinematic composition with clean separation at the exact horizontal
and vertical centre, ready to crop into four independent frames.

1. A distant exoplanet crossing a glowing star while an elegant observatory
   instrument separates the planet's atmospheric light into a subtle rainbow
   spectrum.
2. An abstract atmospheric biosignature visualization with flowing teal, blue
   and restrained warm-orange molecular forms representing oxygen, methane and
   water around a planet; communicate evidence and uncertainty without labels.
3. A harmonious scientific composition showing rust-red Mars beside the
   blue-white cracked ice of Europa or Enceladus, with a magnified translucent
   ice grain containing one tiny tasteful microbe.
4. Earth receiving a faint structured scientific signal that resolves into one
   delicate luminous line on a graph-like plane, while a distant spacecraft
   remains only a tiny silhouette.

Use premium hand-painted editorial explainer animation, simplified graphic
shapes, subtle paper grain, elegant curved motion motifs and a sophisticated
adult science-documentary treatment. Keep the palette deep navy, petrol teal,
desaturated cyan, starlight cream and restrained warm-orange accents. Leave
calm negative space in the lower-right for the separately composited Orbit
mascot.

No characters, robots, people, mascots, text, letters, numbers, logos,
watermarks, humanoid aliens, UI elements or repeated compositions.
