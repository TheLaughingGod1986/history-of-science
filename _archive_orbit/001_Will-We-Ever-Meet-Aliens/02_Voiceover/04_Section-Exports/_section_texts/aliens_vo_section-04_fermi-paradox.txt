Which brings us to the Fermi Paradox — named after physicist Enrico Fermi, who allegedly asked, during lunch, a version of: if alien civilisations are common… where are they?

No radio greetings. No city lights on exoplanets. No obvious megastructures. No visiting fleets.

Just silence — or at least, silence as far as we can tell.

That silence is the problem at the heart of this story. And it has inspired dozens of explanations. Some are hopeful. Some are unsettling. Most are incomplete.
