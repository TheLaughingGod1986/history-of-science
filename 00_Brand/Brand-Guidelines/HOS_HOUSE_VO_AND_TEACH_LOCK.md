# HOS house lock — VO clarity + teach (7 Sep 2026)

**Source:** Ben, after HOS 002 Part 03 KEEP. Applies to **Part 04+** and every later film. Not a remint of Part 03.

## Audience

- Educational for **any age** — child-obvious, phone-first.
- Easy to follow; information quick to ingest; catchy; engaged the whole video.
- No lecture padding. No Orbit rules.

## Scripts must be clearer

Ben: he is **not following the VO** when meaning is thin or abstract.

**Write so a first-time viewer can say the one idea out loud after each beat:**
- Name the thing (chemical / element / tool / person) in plain words.
- Say **what it is** and **why it mattered** in the same breath.
- Prefer concrete nouns over poetry when teaching (poetry OK as garnish after the fact is clear).
- One idea at a time; beat change before attention drops.
- Hook in the first seconds still stands.

## Picture must not lag meaning

House: Animistry labels follow the **spoken word**. Picture must land **with** the VO beat (not 5–10s late).

- If VO says the thing, the hero prop/action is on screen in that window.
- Labels cue with spoken terms (1–4 words, side, elegant).
- Do not let a later plate “explain” a line the viewer already heard and forgot.

## Teach the science (periodic table films)

For 002 Part 04+ (and similar):
- Teach **chemicals** and the **periodic table** in words a newcomer gets.
- Always answer **why the discovery was groundbreaking** — what became possible that was impossible before.
- User-friendly: show the ruler, the gap, the prediction — not only the mood of the hall.
