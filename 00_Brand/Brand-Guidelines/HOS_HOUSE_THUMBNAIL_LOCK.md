## Rules (must)

1. **YouTube TV friendly** — readable at living-room distance; large focal subject; sparse text.
2. **Evoke questions and/or emotions** — mute scroll should feel a question or a felt beat, not a flat diagram dump.
3. **More references** — clear story anchors the watcher already knows or can guess (object, era, proof plate, named beat).
4. **Reduce clutter** — one main read; kill busy backgrounds, stacked labels, helix/prop noise.
5. **Main character obvious** — if a person/Explorer/hand/plate is the hero, it must win the frame in the first glance.
6. **More text variations** — when text is used, draft several short options (1–4 words / Didot-adjacent); pick for TV readability, not keyword stuffing.
