## Studio (plate library)

1. **Plate board:** `07_Edit-Project/parts/part-NN_plates_v01.json`, one plate per VO beat with `vo_land`, `prompt`, `explorer`, `side_label`, plus the part's `forbidden` and `always_fails`.
2. **Seed** each plate from the last KEEP plate in the same set (and the Explorer reference when he is in it). Frame lamps so the underside bulb and shade cup are not visible.
3. **One plate at a time until the first KEEP,** then mint the rest in parallel.
4. **After two FAILs with the same framing,** change the framing or start frame, not just the prompt.
5. **Plate UAT on continuous playback** (start, middle, end, crop-reject), never stills. **Assemble the rough cut last,** when every plate has passed.
6. **Flow only; no paint-patching** of Explorer, desks or light sources.
7. **Auth:** Mac Mini CDP on `benoats@googlemail.com` (Ultra). Stop and report on a passkey or sign-out.
8. **Model tier:** Veo 3.1 **Quality** for fragile light (lamps, flasks, glows, emissive); **Fast** only for low-risk garnish. No Seedance, Omni or Kling.

## Prompt language

- Continuity: "same <place> <year> set". **Never "same DNA" or "lab DNA"** in a Veo prompt.
- Every prompt: Animistry premium 3D cartoon · silent · no readable text · no Orbit · continuous motion.

## Always fails (put in every prompt and every UAT)

- lava drip / molten bead; flame, smoke or spit from a lamp; visible underside bulb or shade cup
- unfinished flat cards; blank or garbled card text
- unfinished Explorer hair, melted face, black dots in the scalp; Explorer without round glasses
- horizontal ghosting or double exposure in late shots
- DNA helix (unless the film is about DNA)
- faced or cute microbes; gore
- the Orbit robot; Ken Burns or still push

## Teach and picture (script → plates)

- **Silent-readable:** each plate shows what its `vo_land` says, so the beat reads with the sound off.
- **Side labels:** white Didot italic, 1–4 words, one at a time, when the VO introduces a person, place/year, tool, idea or why-it-matters turn. Listed in the board's `side_label`.
- **Clear before clever:** name the thing, say what it is and why it mattered.
