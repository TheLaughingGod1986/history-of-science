## Rule

Every plate must **portray what the narrator is saying** so a watcher understands the beat with **sound OFF**.

- **VO-literal action on screen** — the picture tells the same story as the spoken line in that window.
- Labels and teach cards **stay** (teaching density lock) but **do not replace** clear picture storytelling.
- If the VO names a cover, a glow, a test, a hand, a ring — that action/prop must read without audio.
- Fail: narration-only storytelling (pretty lab while VO explains something you cannot see); labels carrying the whole teach while the plate is generic.
