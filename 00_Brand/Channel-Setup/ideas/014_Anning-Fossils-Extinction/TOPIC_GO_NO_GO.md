## 2. Topic Fit & Packaging (`HOS_STRATEGY.md` & `THUMBNAIL_AND_TITLE_RULES.md`)

- **The lane:** *A familiar thing, and the moment we found out the truth about it.*
  - **Familiar thing:** A spiral ammonite fossil on a beach or a museum skeleton — and the everyday knowledge that whole kinds of animals can go extinct.
  - **Person & room:** Twelve-year-old **Mary Anning** (1799–1847) and her brother **Joseph Anning** chipping through storm-slipped Blue Lias shale on the cliffs of **Lyme Regis, Dorset** (and her cramped Broad Street fossil shop), paired in back-and-forth portrait cuts with **Georges Cuvier** (1769–1832) in Paris and **William Conybeare** & **William Buckland** in London.
  - **Moment of proof:** Uncovering the first complete **17-foot (5.2 m) *Ichthyosaurus*** skeleton (1811–1812, with dinner-plate-sized bony sclerotic eye rings, dolphin snout, crocodile teeth, fish vertebrae and four paddles) and the first complete **9-foot *Plesiosaurus*** (10 December 1823, with **35 neck vertebrae** — five times as many as a giraffe's 7), proving beyond doubt that whole worlds of creatures had vanished from Earth, then cracking open "bezoar stones" (coprolites, 1824–1829) and fossil ink sacs to reconstruct the first prehistoric food chain.
  - **Midpoint twist (~60%):** When drawings of the 35-vertebra *Plesiosaurus* reached Paris in 1823, **Georges Cuvier** suspected it was a forged monster stitched together from different animals — until Buckland and Conybeare verified the unbroken stone slab at the **Geological Society of London on 20 February 1824**. Cuvier admitted his mistake and became Anning's customer, yet the Geological Society barred women from even entering the room until 1919, and Sir Everard Home's six Royal Society papers (from 1814) never printed her name.
- **Schedule & hand-off continuity:** Airs Thu 31 Dec 2026 (New Year's Eve), bridging `013` *Darwin & Wallace* (17 Dec) / `012` *Mendel* (24 Dec) and handing off at the end directly to our already-produced dinosaur extinction film `010` (*What Killed the Dinosaurs Was Hidden in a Line of Clay*, public 3 Dec 2026 — *"what kind of catastrophe could wipe out the dinosaurs across the entire planet in a single day?"*).
- **Working title pair (for 2-way Test & Compare):**
  - **Title A (Main — Subject + hook, §1 rule 11, 51 chars):** *Extinction: The 12-Year-Old Who Dug Up a Sea Dragon*
  - **Title B (Test & Compare — Search question/promise + twist, §1 rule 2, 48 chars):** *How a 12-Year-Old Girl Proved Extinction Is Real*
  - **Title C (Alt — Neighbour noun lead, 56 chars):** *Fossils: The Sea Monsters That Proved Extinction Is Real*
- **Thumbnail concept:** One hero object — the 4-foot stone *Ichthyosaurus* skull and huge bony eye ring gleaming in dark wet Blue Lias cliff shale as twelve-year-old Mary Anning's small geological hammer chips it free under a stormy Lyme Regis sky; Explorer small at lower left; 2–3 words of painted serif cover text (*BEFORE DINOSAURS* or *THE LOST OCEAN*, never repeating the title).
