For two thousand years, doctors thought cancer was caused by dark "black bile", chimney soot (`discovered in seventeen seventy-five`), or an infectious parasite — so when thirty-one-year-old **Peyton Rous** in New York made his announcement on the **twenty-first of January nineteen eleven**, the medical world refused to believe him!

A farmer had brought Rous a hen with a connective-tissue tumour. Rous ground up the tumour, poured it through a **Berkefeld filter** that blocked every cell and bacterium, and injected the clear liquid into healthy hens. Those hens grew **identical tumours (`Rous Sarcoma Virus`)**! Experts dismissed it for decades — forcing Rous to wait **fifty-five years**, until **nineteen sixty-six at age eighty-seven**, for his Nobel Prize!

By **nineteen seventy**, **Peter Vogt** had shown that one gene inside that virus — **`src`** — turned cells cancerous. Everyone assumed `src` was an alien viral weapon — until the **eleventh of March nineteen seventy-six** in *Nature*!

At UCSF in San Francisco, **Michael Bishop**, **Harold Varmus**, and **Dominique Stehelin** built a radioactive DNA probe for `src` and dipped it into the DNA of **completely healthy, virus-free birds and mammals**. To their shock, **`c-src` was sitting inside every healthy cell on Earth** (`winning the nineteen eighty-nine Nobel Prize`)! An ancient virus had simply **stolen a normal wound-healing accelerator gene (`a proto-oncogene`)** and jammed the gas pedal down (`confirmed in human tumours in nineteen eighty-two when Robert Weinberg found a single letter typo in the human HRAS gene`)!

If a single letter typo can jam a cell's gas pedal to the floor, **why doesn't every person develop thousands of tumours in childhood** — and how did a **paediatrician in Texas in April nineteen seventy-one** prove that every cell also carries **two emergency brake cables**?

When Knudson plotted the age of diagnosis on a graph, the **inherited cases fell on a straight one-hit line**, while the **non-inherited cases followed a two-hit curve**! Knudson saw why: every retinal cell inherits **two independent brake cables (`the RB1 gene`)** — one from your mother and one from your father. Children with inherited retinoblastoma are born with **brake cable number one already cut in every cell**, so a single random mutation (`Hit Two`) cuts the backup cable. In everyone else, the **exact same cell must suffer two independent one-in-a-million lightning strikes** to sever both brake cables (`proven in nineteen eighty-three and when Stephen Friend, Robert Weinberg, and Thaddeus Dryja cloned RB1 on the sixteenth of October nineteen eighty-six`)!

Whenever radiation or copying errors crack a cell's DNA, **`p53`** halts the cell cycle for repairs — and if the DNA is broken beyond repair, `p53` **orders the damaged cell to self-destruct (`apoptosis`)**! That is why **`p53` is mutated or broken in more than fifty percent of all human cancers**!

And `p53` solved a famous riddle proposed by **Richard Peto** in **nineteen seventy-seven** (**`Peto's Paradox`**): a **five-ton elephant** has **a hundred times more cells than a human** — yet **fewer than five percent of elephants die of cancer**, compared to up to **twenty-five percent of humans**!
