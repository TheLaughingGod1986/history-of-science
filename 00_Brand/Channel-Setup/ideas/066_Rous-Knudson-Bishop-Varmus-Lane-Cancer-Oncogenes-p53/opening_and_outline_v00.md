### PART 02: 21 January 1911 – 11 March 1976 — Peyton Rous's 55-Year Wait & Bishop and Varmus's Proto-Oncogene Shock (`"We Have Met the Enemy and He Is Us"`) (1:25–3:10)
- **Cold open (why for two thousand years doctors thought cancer was either dark "black bile", chemical soot (`Percivall Pott, 1775`), or an infectious parasite — and why scientists laughed when a 31-year-old researcher claimed a sub-microscopic virus could trigger a solid tumour):** **Rockefeller Institute, New York, 21 January 1911 & UCSF, San Francisco, 11 March 1976**.
- **21 January 1911 (*J. Exp. Med.*) — Peyton Rous's porcelain filter & the `Rous Sarcoma Virus` (`RSV`):**
  - When a Long Island poultry breeder brought a barred Plymouth Rock hen with a spindle-cell sarcoma to **Peyton Rous** (1879–1970), Rous ground up a tiny piece of the tumour, passed it through a **Berkefeld filter** that blocked every cancer cell and bacterium (`064`), and injected the crystal-clear filtrate into healthy chickens.
  - To the shock of medicine (**21 January 1911**, *Journal of Experimental Medicine*), the healthy chickens grew identical tumours! Sceptics dismissed it as a weird chicken quirk — forcing Rous to wait **55 years** (`until 1966, at age 87!`) to receive the Nobel Prize!
- **11 March 1976 (*Nature*) — Stehelin, Varmus, Bishop & Vogt unmask `c-src` (`Proto-Oncogenes` inside healthy DNA):**
  - By 1970, **Peter Vogt** had isolated mutant `RSV` viruses that showed a single gene — **`src`** (for sarcoma) — was required to turn a cell cancerous, while **Howard Temin** and **David Baltimore** (**1970**, `1975 Nobel Prize`) discovered **`reverse transcriptase`**, showing how RNA retroviruses stitch their genes into host DNA.
  - Everyone assumed `src` was a foreign viral weapon. At **UCSF in 1975–1976**, **J. Michael Bishop**, **Harold Varmus**, and French postdoc **Dominique Stehelin** built a radioactive DNA probe matching the `src` gene and dipped it into the DNA of **completely healthy, virus-free chickens, ducks, quails, and emus**!
  - On **11 March 1976** (*Nature*), they dropped a thunderbolt on biology: the **`src` gene (`c-src`) was sitting inside every normal bird, mammal, and human cell on Earth**! Millions of years ago, an ancestor of Peyton Rous's virus had simply **accidentally picked up a normal cellular growth-switch gene (`a proto-oncogene`)** and mutated it into a permanently switched-on **`oncogene`** (`winning Bishop and Varmus the 1989 Nobel Prize, and confirmed in non-viral human tumours in 1982 when Robert Weinberg, Mariano Barbacid & Geoffrey Cooper found a single G-to-T typo in the human HRAS proto-oncogene`)!
- *Markers:* `· WHO: Peter K. Vogt (born 1932) — German-American virologist and geneticist · WHO: Robert A. Weinberg (born 1942) — American cancer biologist · WHEN: 1775 · WHEN: 21 January 1911 · WHEN: 1966 · WHEN: 1970 · WHEN: 11 March 1976 · WHEN: 1982 · WHEN: 1989 · FACT: Cancer genes are our own genes · DATA: inked Stehelin, Varmus, Bishop & Vogt (11 March 1976, Nature 260: 170–173) radioactive cDNA hybridization curve on period paper showing viral v-src probe binding strongly to uninfected normal chicken, duck, quail, turkey, and emu cellular DNA (proving c-src is a conserved vertebrate proto-oncogene, plus Weinberg/Barbacid 1982 single G->T codon 12 mutation in human HRAS) · SCALE: Peyton Rous's virus didn't bring an alien poison into the cell — like a car thief who hot-wires your own ignition, it stole a normal growth gene that every healthy cell uses to heal wounds and welded the gas pedal to the floor`
- **Ends on:** If a single letter typo can jam a cell's gas pedal (`oncogene`) to the floor, why doesn't every person develop thousands of tumours before childhood ends — and how did a **paediatrician in Texas in April 1971** prove that every cell also carries **two emergency brake cables**?

### PART 03: April 1971 & October 1986 — Alfred Knudson's "Two-Hit" Equation & the Discovery of `Tumour Suppressor` Brakes — Midpoint Twist (3:10–4:55)
- **Cold open ("Case closed — cancer is just a dominant growth gene with a stuck gas pedal! Wrong! Fuse a cancerous cell with a normal healthy cell, and the hybrid cell STOPS being cancerous — because normal cells carry emergency brake genes that override the stuck gas pedal!"):** **MD Anderson Hospital, Houston, Texas, April 1971 & Massachusetts Eye and Ear / Whitehead Institute, Boston, October 1986**.
- **1969 — Henry Harris's cell-fusion puzzle:**
  - When Oxford cell biologist **Henry Harris** (**1969**, *Nature*) fused malignant tumour cells with normal healthy cells, the hybrid cells **stopped forming tumours**! Something inside the healthy cell was actively **suppressing** cancer (`a brake`).
- **April 1971 (*PNAS*) — Alfred Knudson's "Two-Hit" Retinoblastoma Equation:**
  - In Houston in **April 1971** (*Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences*), paediatric geneticist **Alfred G. Knudson Jr.** (1922–2016) studied **48 children** with **retinoblastoma**, a rare tumour of the retina that comes in two forms: **inherited** (striking **both eyes** in infants) and **sporadic / non-inherited** (striking **only one eye** at a later age).
  - When Knudson graphed the fraction of cases still undiagnosed against age on a semi-log plot, the **inherited cases fell on a straight one-hit exponential line (`k·t`)**, while the **non-inherited cases followed a two-hit curve (`t²`)**!
  - Knudson realised why: every retinal cell inherits **two copies (`alleles`) of a safety-brake gene (`RB1`)** — one from your mother and one from your father. As long as **even one brake cable works**, the cell stops dividing when it should!
  - A child with inherited retinoblastoma is born with **brake cable #1 already cut in every cell** (`Hit 1`), so a single random mutation (`Hit 2`) in any retinal cell cuts the remaining cable and triggers a tumour. In everyone else, the **exact same single cell must suffer two independent lightning strikes (`~1 in a million × 1 in a million`)** to cut both brake cables (`proven at the DNA level by Webster Cavenee in 1983 and when Stephen Friend, René Bernards, Robert Weinberg & Thaddeus Dryja cloned the first tumour-suppressor gene, RB1, on 16 October 1986`)!
- *Markers:* `· WHO: Thaddeus P. Dryja (born 1940) — American ophthalmologist and geneticist · WHEN: 1969 · WHEN: April 1971 · WHEN: 1983 · WHEN: 16 October 1986 · FACT: Two brake cables in every cell · DATA: inked Knudson (April 1971, PNAS 68: 820–823) semi-logarithmic retinoblastoma survival curve on period paper plotting 48 bilateral (hereditary, one-hit linear exponential log S = -kt, mean 3 tumors) vs unilateral (non-hereditary, two-hit quadratic curve, 1 tumor) cases, proving recessive two-hit inactivation of both maternal and paternal RB1 alleles · SCALE: having two copies of every tumour-suppressor gene is like flying on an airliner with twin backup hydraulic brakes — if random chance cuts one cable in a million cells, the backup cable still stops the plane, unless lightning strikes the exact same cell twice`
- **Ends on:** `RB1` was only the first brake cable. Seven years earlier — in **May 1979** — four labs had stumbled upon a mysterious protein weighing **53,000 daltons (`p53`)** that they initially mistook for an accelerator. How did **`p53`** turn out to be the **master guardian of the entire human genome**?

### PART 04: May 1979, April 1989 & October 2015 — `p53` ("The Guardian of the Genome") & Why Giant Elephants Almost Never Get Cancer (`Peto's Paradox`) (4:55–6:45)
- **Cold open (how a protein discovered in 1979 was mistaken for a cancer accelerator for ten years because scientists had accidentally cloned a mutated copy — until they realised wild-type `p53` is the master emergency brake broken in over half of all human cancers):** **Imperial Cancer Research Fund, London & Princeton University, March–May 1979, Johns Hopkins, April 1989, and University of Utah / Chicago, October 2015**.
- **March–May 1979 (*Nature* & *Cell*) & April 1989 (*Science*) — From mistaken oncogene to "The Guardian of the Genome":**
  - In **March–May 1979**, **David Lane & Lionel Crawford** in London (*Nature*), **Daniel Linzer & Arnold Levine** in Princeton (*Cell*), and **Lloyd Old & Albert DeLeo** in New York (*PNAS*) discovered a cellular protein of **53 kilodaltons (`p53`)** bound to viral tumour proteins.
  - Because early labs unknowingly cloned **mutated `p53`** from tumour lines, they thought `p53` was an oncogene — until **Arnold Levine (`1989`)** and **Bert Vogelstein & Suzanne Baker (`14 April 1989`, *Science*)** proved that healthy **wild-type `p53`** on **chromosome 17p** is actually the body's **master tumour-suppressor brake** (`named "The Guardian of the Genome" by David Lane in 1992`)!
  - Whenever UV light (`018`), X-rays (`003`), or copying errors damage a cell's DNA, **`p53`** jumps into action: first it switches on **`p21`** to **pause the cell cycle** so DNA-repair crews can fix the break; if the damage is too severe, `p53` pulls the ultimate emergency cord — **ordering the damaged cell to self-destruct (`apoptosis`)** so it can never become a tumour!
- **1977 & 8 October 2015 (*JAMA*) — Solving Richard Peto's Paradox (`Why 5-ton elephants have <5% cancer mortality`):**
  - In **1977**, Oxford epidemiologist **Richard Peto** pointed out a baffling puzzle (**`Peto's Paradox`**): if every dividing cell has a small chance of mutating into cancer, then a **5-ton elephant (`with 100× more cells than a human`)** or a **100-ton bowhead whale (`with 1,000× more cells`)** should die of cancer in infancy — yet **fewer than `4.8%` of elephants die of cancer**, compared to **`~11–25%` of humans**!
  - On **8 October 2015** (*JAMA*), paediatric oncologist **Joshua Schiffman**, **Carlo Maley**, and evolutionary biologist **Vincent Lynch** sequenced elephant DNA and found the answer: while humans carry just **1 `TP53` gene (`2 alleles`)**, elephants evolved **20 copies (`40 alleles`: 1 ancestral gene plus 19 retrogenes)**! When elephant cells suffer DNA damage, they trigger **`p53`-driven self-destruction (`apoptosis`) at more than twice the rate of human cells** — eliminating pre-cancerous cells before they can ever divide!
- *Markers:* `· WHO: Bert Vogelstein (born 1949) — American oncologist and cancer geneticist · WHO: Richard Peto (born 1943) — British statistician and epidemiologist · WHEN: March 1979 · WHEN: May 1979 · WHEN: 14 April 1989 · WHEN: 1992 · WHEN: 1977 · WHEN: 8 October 2015 · FACT: Elephants carry 40 p53 alleles · DATA: inked Vogelstein (14 April 1989, Science 244: 217–221) chromosome 17p TP53 two-hit loss & Schiffman/Lynch (8 October 2015, JAMA 314: 1850–1860) ionizing-radiation lymphocyte apoptosis chart on period paper showing elephant cells (40 TP53 alleles) undergoing p53-mediated apoptosis at >2x the rate of healthy human cells (2 TP53 alleles) and >5x the rate of Li-Fraumeni syndrome cells (1 TP53 allele) · SCALE: instead of repairing borderline DNA damage and risking a typo, an elephant cell with forty p53 guardians hits the self-destruct button the instant its DNA is scratched`
- **Ends on:** If every one of your **thirty-seven trillion cells** carries the **exact same growth genes and brake genes**, how did **one single fertilised egg cell** know when to divide, when to stop, and **where to build your head, your heart, and your ten fingers** in the first place?

### PART 05: How Targeted Cancer Drugs Lock the Stuck Pedal — & How One Single Egg Cell Knows Where to Build Your Head and Hands (6:45–8:15)
- **Cold open (the misconception that knowing cancer comes from our own mutated genes means we can only poison all dividing cells indiscriminately with chemotherapy — when discovering specific oncogenes like `BCR-ABL` and `HER2` allowed scientists like Brian Druker in 1998–2001 to design molecular keys like `imatinib / Gleevec` that snap directly into the stuck accelerator pocket and switch it off!):** For decades, people feared that if cancer cells are our own cells, we could only fight them with blunt radiation or chemotherapy that harms healthy dividing cells too. Yet discovering **exact oncogene accelerators** (`Bishop & Varmus, 1976`) and **immune checkpoints** (`James Allison & Tasuku Honjo, 2018 Nobel Prize, building on 065`) let scientists design **precision molecular keys** (like **Brian Druker's 2001 `imatinib / Gleevec`**, which fits the stuck `BCR-ABL` kinase pocket in chronic myeloid leukaemia and turned a fatal cancer into a >83% ten-year survival rate) and **checkpoint therapies** that take the brakes off your own T-cells (`065`)!
- **After-act hand-off (7:45–8:15):**
  - Every oncogene and tumour-suppressor gene we just met is a **normal embryonic development gene** whose job was to build your body before you were born.
  - That raises the deepest riddle in all of biology: since every cell in an embryo inherits the **exact same DNA instruction book (`058`)**, **how does one microscopic, round fertilised egg cell know which end is your head, which end is your tail, and where to sprout arms instead of legs**?
  - Why did 22-year-old German student **Hilde Mangold in May 1921** tie a **baby's blond hair** around a newt embryo, transplant a tiny speck of tissue (**`The Organizer`**), and watch it command belly cells to build a **complete second spine and head**?
  - And how did **Edward Lewis's four-winged fruit flies (1978)** and **Christiane Nüsslein-Volhard, Eric Wieschaus, Matthew Scott & Walter Gehring's 1980–1984 discovery of the `180-letter Homeobox` (`Hox` genes)** prove that **flies, mice, and humans still use the exact same 500-million-year-old GPS map** to build their bodies (`067` candidate *How Does a Single Fertilised Egg Know Where to Put Your Head, Heart & Hands (`Embryology, the Spemann-Mangold Organizer & Homeobox / Hox Genes`)?*)?
