For more than two hundred years after **Robert Hooke in sixteen sixty-five** saw thick wooden cork walls, biologists argued over whether soft animal cells even *had* an outer membrane — because a five-nanometre skin is **forty times thinner than the wavelength of visible light**!

Then in **eighteen ninety-five** in Zurich, **Ernest Overton** — a distant cousin of Charles Darwin — tested over five hundred chemical dyes on living cells. Water-loving salts bounced off the invisible boundary, whereas **fat-soluble molecules** slipped straight inside (`and in eighteen ninety-nine Overton showed that the faster a chemical dissolves in olive oil, the faster it crosses into a cell`). Every living cell had to be wrapped in an invisible coat of **`lipids`**!

How many molecules thick was that fat coat? Building on **Irving Langmuir's nineteen seventeen** trough — which floats oil molecules on water in a layer **one molecule thick** — Dutch paediatrician **Evert Gorter** and chemist **François Grendel** in Leiden chose mammalian **red blood cells** (`which have no nucleus and no inner membranes, so all their fat comes from the outer skin`). On the **first of April nineteen twenty-five**, they extracted the fats from counted red blood cells with acetone, spread them into a single layer on water with a silk thread, and measured the area: it was **exactly two to one**! The cell membrane is a **two-molecule `phospholipid bilayer`** with oily tails pointing inward and water-loving heads facing out (`and as Bar, Deamer, and Cornwell revealed in nineteen sixty-six, Gorter and Grendel's acetone missed a third of the fat while their dried slides underestimated the cell area by a third — two mistakes that cancelled out to give the exact right answer!`).

Wait — a pure oil bilayer is a waterproof wall that blocks charged ions like **sodium and potassium**. So how do your nerves fire electrical sparks — and why did a Danish scientist in **February nineteen fifty-seven** boil **twenty-five thousand shore crabs**?

Case closed — the cell membrane is a rigid, motionless sandwich with proteins smeared flat on the outside like butter on toast! Wrong! Between **nineteen fifty-seven and nineteen seventy-two**, scientists proved that the membrane is a **two-dimensional liquid sea** — and that giant protein machines reach right through it, burning a third of your body's energy to pump ions against the tide!

Since **nineteen thirty-five**, textbooks had drawn cell proteins plastered flat over the outside of the lipid bilayer. Yet physiologists knew that inside every living cell, **potassium ions** are kept **thirty times higher** than outside, while **sodium ions** are kept **ten times lower**. Something in the membrane was actively pumping! At Aarhus University in Denmark, **Jens Christian Skou** ground up the leg nerves of **twenty-five thousand green shore crabs** — making the entire physiology building smell like a fish market! In **February nineteen fifty-seven** (`winning the nineteen ninety-seven Nobel Prize`), Skou isolated a membrane-spanning enzyme — **`Na⁺/K⁺-ATPase`, the sodium-potassium pump** — that burns one molecule of **`ATP`** to fire **three sodium ions out** and pull **two potassium ions in**, charging the electrical battery of every nerve and muscle in your body!

And were those proteins locked in place? In **March nineteen seventy**, **Larry Frye** and **Michael Edidin** tagged mouse membrane proteins **green** and human proteins **red**, then fused the two cells together: within **forty minutes at body temperature**, the red and green proteins were completely mixed like marbles rolling on a pond! On the **eighteenth of February nineteen seventy-two** in *Science*, **Jonathan Singer** and **Garth Nicolson** named it the **`Fluid Mosaic Model`**: the membrane is a **liquid phospholipid sea** where protein pumps and channels float like **icebergs**!

Yet one giant mystery remained: if the core of that membrane is **water-repelling oil**, how does **water** gush into red blood cells and kidney tubules **a hundred times faster** than oil allows? Why did **six frog eggs in Baltimore in October nineteen ninety-one** suddenly **explode like popcorn**?

For seventy years, biophysicists argued over whether cells had dedicated water pipes — until an American blood doctor hunting for Rhesus antigens injected a mystery protein into six frog eggs, dropped them into distilled water, and watched them blow up in three minutes!

In **nineteen eighty-eight** at Johns Hopkins, haematologist **Peter Agre** found a mysterious protein packed inside red blood cells and kidney tubules — two hundred thousand copies per cell — which he called **`CHIP28`**. When his mentor suggested it might be the long-sought cellular water channel, Agre's postdoc **Gregory Preston** on the **ninth of October nineteen ninety-one** injected `CHIP28` genetic instructions into **six frog eggs** (`which normally have tight, waterproof skins`), left six control eggs untouched, and dropped both groups into **distilled water**. The six control eggs sat completely still. The six **`CHIP28`** eggs **swelled up like water balloons and exploded within three minutes**! Published on the **seventeenth of April nineteen ninety-two** in *Science* (`winning Agre the two thousand and three Nobel Prize alongside ion-channel pioneer Roderick MacKinnon`), Agre named the protein **`aquaporin`** — the water plumbing of all living things!

Even better, why doesn't a pipe for water let acid **protons** leak through and short-circuit the cell? In **October two thousand** in *Nature*, Agre's team revealed that an aquaporin is an hourglass tunnel just **`two point eight angstroms` wide** — wide enough for **one water molecule at a time**! Halfway down, an electric dipole (**`the NPA motif`**) grabs each water molecule and **flips it upside down mid-flight** — breaking the proton wire so **three billion water molecules a second** race through while **zero protons** can cross!

Where in your body do **aquaporins** and **sodium-potassium pumps** perform their biggest everyday miracle? Right inside your two fist-sized **kidneys** — which filter **one hundred and eighty litres of water out of your blood every single day** and rescue **ninety-nine percent of it**!
