How do you measure the age of a planet when no human was there to watch it form?

In eighteen sixty-two, Britain's most celebrated physicist, William Thomson — later **Lord Kelvin** — turned that question into a calculation. Measuring how temperature rises as miners dig deeper underground, Kelvin calculated how long it would take an initially molten globe of rock and iron to cool to its present crust — and declared that the Earth could be **no more than twenty to forty million years old**!

Charles Darwin called Lord Kelvin's number one of his "sorest troubles" — because evolution by natural selection and the slow building of rock strata needed *hundreds of millions or billions* of years!

Why was Lord Kelvin's calculation so wrong? Because he assumed the Earth had **no hidden source of heat**!

Then, in eighteen ninety-six to nineteen oh three, Henri Becquerel and Marie and Pierre Curie discovered **radioactivity** — and proved that radioactive radium continuously gives off heat! Thirty-two-year-old New Zealand physicist Ernest Rutherford realised two revolutionary things at once. First, radioactive uranium and thorium deep inside the Earth act as a **nuclear furnace**, keeping the planet hot for billions of years! Second, radioactive uranium decays into stable **lead** at an unalterable **half-life** — four point four seven billion years for Uranium-two-thirty-eight, and seven hundred and four million years for Uranium-two-thirty-five! Count the ratio of uranium to lead inside a rock crystal, and you can read the rock's exact age like an atomic hourglass!

On the evening of the twentieth of May nineteen oh four, Rutherford walked onto the stage of the Royal Institution in London — and spotted seventy-nine-year-old Lord Kelvin in the front row! To Rutherford's relief, Kelvin fell fast asleep — until the exact moment Rutherford reached the age of the Earth, when the old man suddenly sat bolt upright and glared at him!

Thinking in a flash, Rutherford announced that *Lord Kelvin himself* had prophetically warned his cooling calculation held true **"provided no new source of heat was discovered"** — and held up a black lump of radioactive **pitchblende**! Kelvin beamed with delight! Soon after, Bertram Boltwood in nineteen oh seven and young English geologist Arthur Holmes in nineteen eleven to nineteen thirteen dated ancient rocks past **one point six billion years**!

But if Earth's oldest surviving rocks had melted and recycled since the planet was born, why couldn't anyone find the **true birth date of the Earth itself** — until a Chicago professor handed a graduate student a piece of a fallen meteorite?

Why couldn't geologists find a rock on Earth from the day our planet formed? Because volcanoes, erosion, and moving continents have melted and recycled Earth's original baby crust!

In nineteen forty-eight at the University of Chicago, geochemist Harrison Brown realised the secret: **meteorites** are leftover rubble from the birth of the Solar System that never melted inside a planet! If you compare the lead isotopes inside Earth's rocks against the primordial, uranium-free lead sealed inside an iron meteorite — the **Canyon Diablo meteorite** from Arizona — you can calculate the exact birth date of the Earth!

Brown handed the lead-measuring job to twenty-six-year-old Iowa graduate student Clair Patterson, promising him it would be ***"duck soup"*** — easy — and take just one year!

Case closed? Wrong! For **six exhausting years**, every single blank test Patterson ran had **up to two hundred times too much lead**!

Where was that invisible ghost lead coming from? From the brass pipes, the tap water, the bottled acid, the dust floating in the air, and **even Patterson's own hair and clothes**!

Moving to Caltech in nineteen fifty-two, Patterson tore out the metal plumbing, boiled every quartz beaker in doubly distilled acid, pumped in filtered air under positive pressure, and required plastic suits and shoe covers — **inventing the world's first ultra-clean room**, the ancestor of every microchip clean room on Earth today!

Finally, in autumn nineteen fifty-three, Patterson carried his ultra-pure sample of the Canyon Diablo meteorite to the giant mass spectrometer at Argonne National Laboratory. As the recorder pen traced the lead isotopes, Patterson calculated the true age of the Earth — **four point five five billion years, plus or minus seventy million years**!

Thirty-one-year-old Clair Patterson was so overwhelmed that first night that his heart raced and he drove straight to his mother's house in Iowa and asked her to check him into a hospital because he thought he was having a heart attack!

Having solved the age of the Earth, Patterson couldn't let go of the mystery that had haunted his laboratory for six years: **why was the entire modern world drenched in two hundred times too much toxic lead?**
