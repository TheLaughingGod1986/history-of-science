## The five

### 1. Surgery before anaesthesia: Ether Day (recommended)
- **Familiar thing:** being put to sleep for an operation.
- **Story:** before October 1846, surgeons were judged by speed, because the patient was awake. On 16 October 1846 a Boston dentist, William Morton, put a young man to sleep with ether in front of a hall full of doctors at Massachusetts General Hospital. The surgeon, John Collins Warren, told the room: "Gentlemen, this is no humbug."
- **Twist:** a country doctor in Georgia, Crawford Long, had already done it in 1842 and told no one in print until 1849. Horace Wells tried it with laughing gas in 1845 and was booed out of the same hospital. The men then fought over the credit for the rest of their lives, and Boston's 1868 Ether Monument names none of them (to verify).
- **Neighbours (gate PASS, 5 big):**
  - TED-Ed *How does anesthesia work?* (19.7M views);
  - TED-Ed *What did people do before anesthesia?* (2.6M);
  - TED-Ed *How aspirin was discovered* (3.1M), the medicine-history neighbour;
  - SciShow *We Finally Know How Anesthesia Works* (443k).

  "surgery before anesthesia" and "who invented anesthesia" both autocomplete.
- **Neighbour phrase:** *anesthesia*. The US spelling is what the neighbours and searchers use; the channel writes UK English. **Chief to rule** on the title spelling (the description can carry both).
- **Overlap:** low to medium. 001 Germs covers infection and Lister's spray (the Short "The spray that stopped surgery killing patients"). This film is about pain, not infection, but the operating-theatre setting repeats. Keep Lister out except for one hand-off line.
- **Title shapes (Chief picks):**

### 2. Aspirin: from willow bark to a pill
- **Story:**
  - in 1763 the Reverend Edward Stone wrote to the Royal Society that powdered willow bark eased fevers;
  - in 1897 Felix Hoffmann at Bayer made acetylsalicylic acid;
  - the twist: Arthur Eichengrün later said he had directed the work, and the credit is still argued over.
- **Neighbours (PASS, 4):** TED-Ed *How aspirin was discovered* (3.1M) is on the subject; SciShow *How Aspirin Changed Medicine Forever* has 507k. The other two 1M+ are general physiology.
- **Why not first:** 006 is *The Willow Tree That Was Made of Air* (airs 5 Nov). A second willow film in the same season blurs the channel. Better from early 2027.

### 4. Eratosthenes measures the Earth with a shadow
- **Story:** about 240 BC: no shadow in a well at Syene at noon, a shadow at Alexandria, about 7°, so the Earth is about 50 times that distance around.
- **Neighbours:** the gate PASSes on paper (8–11 big), but nearly all are general TED-Ed *Earth* videos, not this subject. The on-subject results are smaller (Business Insider 790k, Eames Office 150k). Treat it as a weak pass.

### 5. Continental drift: Wegener, mocked and then proved
- **Story:**
  - in 1912 Wegener says the continents move, and geologists laugh;
  - he dies on the Greenland ice in 1930;
  - in the 1950s–60s Marie Tharp's sea-floor maps and sea-floor spreading prove the idea.
- **Neighbours: FAIL** (1–2 big: MinuteEarth *Plate Tectonics Explained* 3.26M and TED-Ed *What happens when continents collide?* 1.88M; National Geographic is 983k). "continental drift wegener" autocompletes.
