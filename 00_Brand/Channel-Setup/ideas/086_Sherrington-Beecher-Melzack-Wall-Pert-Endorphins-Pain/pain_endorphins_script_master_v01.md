On **nineteen November nineteen sixty-five**, they unveiled the **`Gate Control Theory of Pain`**. Before any pain signal from your foot or hand can climb to your brain, it must pass through a relay station in the back of your spinal cord: the **`dorsal horn`** (`the substantia gelatinosa`). Inside that relay sits a neural **gate** of inhibitory cells. When **slow, thin `C` pain fibres** fire alone, they push the spinal gate **wide open**. Yet when you **rub your skin**, **fast, thick `A-beta` touch fibres** (`racing at seventy-five metres per second`) arrive at the spine first and **slam the gate shut** — blocking the slow pain signals! Better still, a **top-down cable from your brain** can lock that same spinal gate shut during emergencies (`explaining Beecher's Anzio soldiers`), while buzzing fast touch nerves with gentle electricity inspired **Patrick Wall** in **nineteen sixty-seven** to invent **`TENS` pain-relief machines**!
