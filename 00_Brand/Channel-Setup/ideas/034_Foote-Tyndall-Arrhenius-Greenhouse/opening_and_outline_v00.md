### PART 02: Fourier's Frozen Earth, Agassiz's Ice Age & Eunice Foote's 1856 Glass Cylinders (1:25–3:05)
- **Cold open (why isn't Earth an ice ball?):** **Paris, 1824–1827 & Neuchâtel, Switzerland, 24 July 1837**. In **1824** (expanded in **1827**), French mathematician **Joseph Fourier** (1768–1830) asked a deceptively simple question: Earth sits **150 million kilometres** from the Sun in the freezing vacuum of space — so what sets our planet's temperature?
- Calculating the balance between incoming sunlight and outgoing heat, Fourier realised Earth should be **far colder (`about −18 °C` / `0 °F`)** than it actually is! Borrowing an experiment by Swiss mountaineer **Horace-Bénédict de Saussure** (who built a wooden box topped with panes of glass to trap solar heat on alpine peaks), Fourier proposed that Earth's atmosphere lets visible light *in*, but blocks **invisible "dark heat" (`chaleur obscure` — infrared radiation, discovered by William Herschel in `018`)** on its way *out*!
- **24 July 1837 — The Ice Age shock:** Ten years later, on **24 July 1837**, 30-year-old Swiss geologist **Louis Agassiz** (1807–1873) shocked the scientific world by proving from scratched bedrock and giant erratic boulders scattered across Europe that the continent had once been buried under **a mile of prehistoric ice — an Ice Age (*Eiszeit*)**!
- That raised an urgent puzzle: if Earth's atmosphere is an insulating blanket, **what gas inside the air could change over geological time to switch an Ice Age on and off?**
- **Summer 1856 & 23 August 1856 — Eunice Newton Foote's sunlit cylinders:** In **Seneca Falls, New York**, 37-year-old **Eunice Newton Foote** (1819–1888) — a neighbour and friend of Elizabeth Cady Stanton who had signed the **1848 Seneca Falls Declaration of Sentiments** and studied science at the Troy Female Seminary — decided to test which gases actually trap the Sun's heat!
  - She took **two identical glass cylinders** (`4 inches` in diameter, `30 inches` long), mounted a mercury thermometer inside each, and used an air pump to test **condensed air vs rarefied (thin) air**, **moist air vs dry air**, and **hydrogen, oxygen, and carbonic acid gas ($CO_2$)**.
  - Placed in the summer sun, the cylinder of **moist air** heated far more than dry air (`108 °F` vs `96 °F`) — and the cylinder filled with **carbonic acid gas ($CO_2$)** gave the highest reading of all (**`125 °F` / `51.7 °C`**, compared to `106 °F` in ordinary air) and took far longer to cool down in the shade!
  - Foote immediately grasped the planetary consequence, writing: *"An atmosphere of that gas would give to our earth a high temperature; and if as some suppose, at one period of its history the air had mixed with it a larger proportion than at present, an increased temperature from its own action, as well as from increased weight, must have necessarily resulted."*
  - Yet on **23 August 1856** at the **AAAS meeting in Albany**, women were not allowed to present papers; Smithsonian Secretary **Joseph Henry** read her paper aloud, the AAAS omitted it from its official *Proceedings* (though it appeared in the **November 1856** *American Journal of Science and Arts*), and her name was erased from climate history for **155 years** until **Raymond Sorenson** rediscovered her paper in **2011**!
- *Markers:* `· WHO: Joseph Fourier (1768–1830) — French mathematician and physicist · WHO: Louis Agassiz (1807–1873) — Swiss-American geologist · WHEN: 1824 · WHEN: 24 July 1837 · WHEN: November 1856 · WHEN: 2011 · FACT: Forgotten for 155 years · DATA: inked thermometer-chart on period paper from Eunice Foote's 1856 paper comparing dry air (96 °F / 35.6 °C) vs damp air (108 °F / 42.2 °C), and ambient air (106 °F / 41.1 °C) vs carbonic acid gas / CO₂ (125 °F / 51.7 °C) in sunlit 30-inch glass cylinders · SCALE: inside a 30-inch glass tube in the New York sun, carbon dioxide climbed 19 degrees Fahrenheit hotter than ordinary air — the first experimental clue to how changing air changes Earth's climate`
- **Ends on:** Foote had proved $CO_2$ traps solar heat inside sunlit glass tubes. Yet physicists soon pointed out a catch: glass itself blocks the invisible infrared heat radiating upward from the dark Earth at night! So how did an Irish mountaineer and physicist in a London basement in **May 1859** build a machine with **rock-salt windows** to test the invisible heat of the Earth itself?

### PART 03: John Tyndall's 1859 Rock-Salt Spectrophotometer & the 99% Shock (3:05–4:50)
- **Cold open:** **The Royal Institution, Albemarle Street, London, 18 May 1859**. Like Louis Agassiz, Irish physicist **John Tyndall** (1820–1893) — **Michael Faraday's (`021`)** protégé and successor at the Royal Institution — was a passionate Alpine mountaineer who spent his summers climbing Swiss glaciers (`including the first ascent of the Weisshorn in 1861`) to figure out how glaciers flow and what caused the Ice Ages!
- Unaware of Eunice Foote's unindexed 1856 paper in America, Tyndall realised that to test Fourier's atmospheric blanket properly, you couldn't use sunlight and glass tubes:
  - Sunlight is mostly **visible light and near-infrared**, which passes through the air to warm the ground;
  - The warm ground then radiates its heat back toward space as **long-wave thermal infrared ("dark heat" from a 15 °C surface or a 100 °C boiling kettle)** — and ordinary glass blocks long-wave infrared completely!
- **May 1859 — The first infrared ratio spectrophotometer:** In the basement of the Royal Institution, Tyndall built a four-foot brass tube sealed at both ends with polished plates of **transparent rock salt (`NaCl`)** — the only solid crystal that lets long-wave infrared heat pass straight through!
  - At one end sat a **Leslie cube** filled with boiling water (`100 °C`), radiating invisible infrared rays down the brass tube toward a sensitive **thermopile** and galvanometer needle (`021`).
  - For weeks in early **1859**, Tyndall pumped pure **oxygen ($O_2$)**, pure **nitrogen ($N_2$)**, and dry air into the tube — and the galvanometer needle barely budged! **99% of Earth's atmosphere is as transparent to infrared heat as a vacuum!**
  - Then, on **18 May 1859**, Tyndall let a trace of **coal gas, carbon dioxide ($CO_2$), and water vapour ($H_2O$)** into the brass tube — and the galvanometer needle **swung wildly across the dial**! He wrote in his lab diary that night: *"Experimented all day; the subject is completely in my hands!"*
  - Why? Because two-atom symmetric molecules like **$O_2$ and $N_2$** have no changing electric dipole when they vibrate, so infrared waves pass right past them — whereas **three-atom molecules like $H_2O$, $CO_2$, and ozone ($O_3$)** bend and stretch asymmetrically, absorbing infrared wavelengths like a tuned radio antenna!
  - As Tyndall told the Royal Institution in **January–February 1861**: water vapour and carbon dioxide act like **"a dam built across abk-flowing stream,"** and without them, *"the warmth of our fields and gardens would pour itself unrequited into space, and the sun would rise upon an island held fast in the iron grip of frost!"*
- *Markers:* `· WHEN: 18 May 1859 · WHEN: 7 February 1861 · FACT: 99% of air traps no heat · DATA: inked spectrophotometer diagram & bar chart on period paper from Tyndall's 1859–1861 Royal Society experiments comparing relative infrared absorption at 1 atm: Nitrogen (1), Oxygen (1), Dry Air (1) vs Carbon Dioxide (90), Nitrous Oxide (355), and Coal Gas / Hydrocarbons (>1,000), alongside humid air absorbing ~70× more thermal radiation than dry air · SCALE: pound for pound, a three-atom molecule of carbon dioxide or water vapour absorbs thousands of times more infrared heat than the two-atom oxygen and nitrogen molecules that make up 99% of every breath you take`
- **Ends on:** Foote and Tyndall had identified the heat-trapping gases in the laboratory. Case closed? **Not yet.** Neither of them had calculated **how many degrees Earth's temperature would actually rise or fall** if you doubled or halved the carbon dioxide in the sky! Who would dare calculate the temperature of the entire planet without a computer — and what personal heartbreak drove him to do **ten thousand sums by hand**?

### PART 04: Svante Arrhenius's 10,000 Hand Calculations & the Coal Twist — Midpoint Twist (4:50–6:40)
- **Cold open ("The human computer in Stockholm"):** **Stockholm, Sweden, Christmas Eve 1894 – April 1896**. In the 1890s, Stockholm physicists led by **Arvid Högbom** (`1894`) debated what could have caused the prehistoric **Ice Ages** and the warm tropical eras when coal swamps grew in the Arctic (`030`).
- Physical chemist **Svante Arrhenius** (1859–1927) — who would soon win the **1903 Nobel Prize in Chemistry** for proving how salts split into charged ions in water — decided to calculate whether changes in **atmospheric carbon dioxide ($CO_2$)** could actually freeze or thaw the globe!
- **1895 — 10,000 to 100,000 pencil calculations during a divorce:** Today, climate scientists run radiative transfer equations on supercomputers. In **1895**, Arrhenius had only a pencil, reams of ledger paper, and infrared moonlight absorption measurements made by **Samuel Pierpont Langley** (`1884–1890`).
  - Why did Arrhenius subject himself to a full year of mind-numbing arithmetic — between **10,000 and 100,000 hand calculations**, working fourteen hours a day through Christmas Eve 1894 and all of 1895?
  - Partly because his brief marriage to his former student **Sofia Rudbeck** was collapsing in **1894–1895**, and burying himself in endless columns of numbers across **every 10-degree latitude band from the Arctic to the Equator** was how he numbed his heartbreak! (He later wrote to a friend how *"unbelievably trifling"* the question seemed beside the sheer mountain of tedious calculations.)
- **April 1896 — The water-vapour feedback & the Ice Age answer:** Published in the *London, Edinburgh, and Dublin Philosophical Magazine* in **April 1896** (*"On the Influence of Carbonic Acid in the Air upon the Temperature of the Ground"*), Arrhenius discovered the key amplifier (**water-vapour feedback**):
  - When a rise in **$CO_2$** warms the air slightly, warmer air evaporates and holds **more water vapour ($H_2O$)** — which is itself a powerful greenhouse gas that amplifies the warming further!
  - Arrhenius calculated that **cutting atmospheric $CO_2$ in half** would cool Europe by **`4 to 5 °C` (`7–9 °F`)** — the exact temperature drop needed to trigger a continental **Ice Age**!
  - Conversely, **doubling atmospheric $CO_2$** would warm the globe by **`5 to 6 °C`** (remarkably close to modern supercomputer climate models pioneered by **Syukuro Manabe and Richard Wetherald in May 1967**, which place equilibrium climate sensitivity near **`~3 °C` [range `2.5–4 °C`]**)!
- **The ironic twist — why Arrhenius cheered for coal burning:** In **1896**, humanity was burning about **500 million tonnes of coal a year** (16 times less than today!). Arrhenius calculated it would take **3,000 years** for human industry to double $CO_2$ — and sitting in freezing Stockholm, he wrote in his **1908** book *Worlds in the Making* that burning fossil coal would prevent the next Ice Age and bless humanity with *"ages with more equable and better climates, especially as regards the colder regions of the earth"*!
- *Markers:* `· WHEN: April 1896 · WHEN: 1903 · WHEN: 1908 · WHEN: May 1967 · FACT: 10,000 sums done by hand · DATA: inked calculation table & curve on period paper from Svante Arrhenius's April 1896 paper showing global temperature change vs atmospheric CO₂ multiplier: 0.67× CO₂ → −3.2 °C; 0.5× CO₂ (halved) → −4 to −5 °C (Ice Age); 1.0× CO₂ → baseline; 2.0× CO₂ (doubled) → +5 to +6 °C (compared with Manabe & Wetherald's 1967 computer model at +2.3 to +3.0 °C) · SCALE: working only with pencil and paper in 1895, Arrhenius calculated a warming curve for doubled carbon dioxide within two degrees of today's fastest supercomputers`
- **Ends on:** Only in **1938** (by British steam engineer **Guy Stewart Callendar**) and from **March 1958** onward (when **Charles David Keeling** began measuring $CO_2$ atop **Mauna Loa Observatory in Hawaii**, watching it climb from **`315 parts per million` in 1958 to over `420 ppm` today**) did scientists realise that humanity wasn't taking 3,000 years to raise $CO_2$ — we were doing it in a single lifetime!

### PART 05: Why a Real Garden Greenhouse Works Differently & the $1 Medical Miracle (6:40–8:15)
- **Cold open (popular misconception debunked):** Even the name **"greenhouse effect"** (coined by Swedish meteorologist **Nils Ekholm** in **1901** and debated by physicist **Robert W. Wood** in **1909**) hides a funny physical twist:
  - A **glass garden greenhouse** stays warm mostly because its glass roof **physically traps warm air inside and stops it rising away by convection** (`if you build a greenhouse with rock-salt panes that let infrared escape, it still gets hot inside because the warm air can't blow away!`);
  - **Earth's atmosphere has no solid roof** — instead, $CO_2$ and water vapour molecules high in the cold upper air absorb outgoing infrared heat and re-radiate it downward, acting like adding an extra wool blanket on your bed until the whole system warms up to restore energy balance!
- **After-act hand-off (7:45–8:15):**
  - And where does the carbon in that atmospheric $CO_2$ go? Green plants (`006`) weave it into **glucose sugar** — the fuel that every cell in your body burns to stay alive, provided a tiny hormone unlocked from the pancreas lets that sugar through the cell door!
  - How did a struggling 29-year-old Canadian war surgeon named **Frederick Banting** wake at **2:00 a.m. on 31 October 1920** to scribble a 25-word idea that solved why the pancreas's own digestive enzymes had destroyed every attempt to isolate **insulin**, how did he, **Charles Best**, and **James Collip** walk down a Toronto hospital ward in **January 1922** waking dying diabetic children from comas, and why did they sell the patent for **one dollar each** (`035` candidate *The $1 Miracle: How Insulin Turned Diabetes From a Death Sentence Into Life*)?
