For two hundred years, British shepherds watched sheep rub their wool raw against fences and stagger with a fatal brain sickness called **`scrapie`** (`proven transmissible between sheep in nineteen thirty-six`). Then in **nineteen fifty-seven**, American virologist **Carleton Gajdusek** and Australian researcher **Michael Alpers** investigated a devastating neurological disease called **`kuru`** among the Fore people of Papua New Guinea. Victims suffered **zero fever and zero immune inflammation**, yet their brains were riddled with microscopic sponge-like holes!

On the **fifth of September nineteen fifty-nine** in *The Lancet*, American veterinary pathologist **William Hadlow** visited a medical exhibit in London, saw photographs of `kuru` brain slices, and immediately wrote to Gajdusek: `kuru` and sheep `scrapie` looked **identical**! On the **nineteenth of February nineteen sixty-six** in *Nature*, Gajdusek, **Clarence Gibbs**, and Alpers proved that `kuru` was a slow transmissible disease tied to mortuary rites (`winning Gajdusek the nineteen seventy-six Nobel Prize as ending the rites eliminated kuru`).

Every virologist assumed `scrapie` and `kuru` were caused by a **"slow virus"**. To measure the size of its DNA or RNA core, radiobiologist **Tikvah Alper** at Hammersmith Hospital in London in **May nineteen sixty-six** and on the **twentieth of May nineteen sixty-seven** in *Nature* blasted the scrapie agent with massive electron beams and **`254-nanometre` ultraviolet light** that shreds DNA and RNA. Every control virus died — **yet the scrapie agent survived untouched**! Four months later, on the **ninth of September nineteen sixty-seven** in *Nature*, British mathematician **John Stanley Griffith** calculated how a **misfolded host protein** could catalyse its own folding without any DNA at all!

Biologists treated Alper and Griffith's protein-only idea as scientific heresy. Who dared to purify the mystery agent in **San Francisco in April nineteen eighty-two** — and what shocking secret did he find when he searched for its gene in **nineteen eighty-five**?

Case closed — every infectious disease is an alien invader that brings its own foreign DNA or RNA into your body! Wrong! In **nineteen eighty-two** and **nineteen eighty-five**, scientists proved that the infectious particle has **zero nucleic acid** — and that the gene that makes it is sitting inside **every single healthy human brain right now**!

After watching a patient die of **`Creutzfeldt-Jakob disease`** in **nineteen seventy-two**, young UCSF neurologist **Stanley Prusiner** spent ten years purifying the scrapie agent from thousands of hamster brains. On the **ninth of April nineteen eighty-two** in *Science*, Prusiner coined the word **`prion`** — short for *proteinaceous infectious particle*: every enzyme or beam that destroyed **DNA or RNA** did **zero damage** to infectivity, whereas every chemical that unfolded or digested **proteins (`like proteinase K`)** wiped infectivity out!

Then came the shock that stunned biology. On the **nineteenth of April nineteen eighty-five** in *Cell*, Prusiner, **Leroy Hood**, and **Charles Weissmann** sequenced the prion protein (`PrP`) and searched for its gene — expecting to find a foreign virus. Instead, the **`PRNP` gene** lit up inside the chromosomes of **every healthy hamster, mouse, and human on Earth**! Why doesn't our own normal prion protein (**`PrP-C`**) harm us? As Prusiner's team proved on the **first of December nineteen ninety-three** in *PNAS*, it is pure **three-dimensional origami**: normal **`PrP-C`** is folded into **forty-three percent springy `alpha-helices`** and just **three percent flat `beta-sheets`**. The diseased prion (**`PrP-Sc`**) has the **exact same amino-acid sequence**, but is misfolded into **forty-three percent flat, rigid `beta-sheets`** that stack into indestructible rods!

Wait — fierce critics still insisted Prusiner's protein was just a bystander and that an invisible virus was hiding in his test tubes! How did **Charles Weissmann** in **Zurich on the second of July nineteen ninety-three** design one genetic experiment on mice that settled the war forever — right in the middle of Britain's **Mad Cow Disease** crisis?

How do you prove beyond a shadow of a doubt that a disease is spread by a falling protein domino and not by a hidden virus? You breed a mouse with **no standing dominoes in its brain**!

On the **fourteenth of May nineteen ninety-two** in *Nature*, **Charles Weissmann**, **Hansruedi Büeler**, and **Adriano Aguzzi** at the University of Zurich deleted the normal prion gene in mice (**`Prnp-zero` knockout mice**) — and the mice grew up completely healthy. Then, on the **second of July nineteen ninety-three** in *Cell*, Weissmann's team injected lethal scrapie prions straight into their brains! If scrapie were a virus carrying its own genes, it would kill the mice anyway. Instead, while every normal mouse died within **twenty-three weeks**, **every single prion-knockout mouse stayed one hundred percent healthy for over five hundred days with zero prion multiplication**! With no normal **`PrP-C` dominoes** standing in the brain, the misfolded **`PrP-Sc` domino** had nothing to knock over!

That proof arrived right in the middle of a medical emergency: starting in **November nineteen eighty-six**, over **one hundred and eighty thousand British cattle** developed **Bovine Spongiform Encephalopathy (`Mad Cow Disease`)** from prion-contaminated cattle feed that had survived rendering heat (`and on the sixth of April nineteen ninety-six in The Lancet, Robert Will and James Ironside identified variant CJD in humans`). Banning contaminated feed stopped the epidemic, and in **October nineteen ninety-seven**, **Stanley Prusiner** won the sole **Nobel Prize**!

Prions showed the terrifying power of a protein's **three-dimensional shape** when it folds **wrong**. But how do **properly folded proteins (`enzymes`)** act as molecular locks that speed up the chemistry of life **a billion times** — and how did **crushed brewer's yeast with zero living cells in Munich in January eighteen ninety-seven** birth modern biochemistry?

People often assume prions are only about rare diseases like `kuru` and `Mad Cow Disease`. In fact, **Stanley Prusiner's** once-ridiculed heresy transformed all of brain science: researchers discovered that harmless yeast cells actually use shape-shifting **yeast prions** as beneficial protein memory, while in conditions like **Alzheimer's** and **Parkinson's**, misfolded proteins spread from neuron to neuron inside the brain by the exact same domino mechanism — giving scientists new ways to block the chain reaction!

Prions revealed what happens when a protein folds into the wrong shape. But how did scientists discover what a protein's three-dimensional shape does when it folds **right** — forming the microscopic molecular machines (**`enzymes`**) that speed up the chemical reactions of life **a billion to a hundred trillion times**? Why did **Louis Pasteur in eighteen fifty-seven** insist that fermenting sugar into alcohol required a mystical **"living vital force"** inside intact yeast cells? How did **Emil Fischer in eighteen ninety-four** propose the **Lock-and-Key (`Schloss und Schlüssel`)** model, and how did **Eduard Buchner in Munich on the eleventh of January eighteen ninety-seven** crush yeast under **three hundred atmospheres** until **zero living cells remained** — and watch the dead juice bubble with carbon dioxide and alcohol (**`Zymase`**)? And how did **one-armed chemist James Sumner in August nineteen twenty-six** prove against a mocking Nobel laureate that enzymes are **pure crystallisable proteins**, before **Daniel Koshland in February nineteen fifty-eight** and **David Phillips on the twenty-second of May nineteen sixty-five** took the first three-dimensional X-ray snapshot of an enzyme snapping bacterial armour in half?

A letter in *The Lancet*, an ultraviolet beam in London, and a knockout mouse in Zurich. Together, they proved that a single folded protein can carry a memory of its own.
