### PART 02: 5 September 1959, 19 February 1966 & May–September 1967 — `Kuru`, Sheep `Scrapie` & Tikvah Alper's Radiation-Proof Heresy (1:25–3:10)
- **Cold open (why for two hundred years Scottish and English shepherds watched sheep rub their wool raw against fences and stagger with a fatal brain sickness called `scrapie` — and why a medical mystery halfway around the world in the highlands of Papua New Guinea suddenly revealed the same microscopic holes in human brains):** **Eastern Highlands, Papua New Guinea & Wellcome Museum, London, 5 September 1959 – May 1967**.
- **5 September 1959 (*The Lancet*) & 19 February 1966 (*Nature*) — William Hadlow's letter & Gajdusek and Alpers's `Kuru` proof:**
  - In **1957**, American paediatrician and virologist **D. Carleton Gajdusek** (1923–2008) and Australian doctor **Vincent Zigas** (soon joined by **Michael Alpers**, born 1934) investigated a fatal trembling neurological disease called **`kuru`** among the Fore people of Papua New Guinea. Strangely, victims had **zero fever, zero inflammation, and zero immune response (`065`)**, yet their brains were riddled with microscopic sponge-like holes (**`spongiform encephalopathy`**).
  - On **5 September 1959** (*The Lancet*), American veterinary pathologist **William Hadlow** visited a Wellcome medical exhibition in London, saw photographs of `kuru` brain slices, and immediately wrote a letter pointing out that they looked **identical** to **`scrapie`** in sheep (`which Jean Cuillé and Paul Chelle had proven transmissible in sheep in 1936 with a 2-year incubation period!`).
  - Inspired by Hadlow, **Gajdusek, Clarence Gibbs, and Michael Alpers** published their landmark proof on **19 February 1966** (*Nature*, winning Gajdusek the **1976 Nobel Prize**): `kuru` was a transmissible infectious disease with a multi-year silent incubation (`spread through mortuary feasts that Fore elders and Alpers helped end, causing kuru to vanish in the younger generation`).
- **May 1966, 20 May 1967 & 9 September 1967 (*Nature*) — Tikvah Alper's UV/electron beam & John Stanley Griffith's protein-only hypothesis:**
  - Every virologist assumed `scrapie` and `kuru` were caused by a **"slow virus"**. To measure the size of that virus's genetic core, South African-born radiobiologist **Tikvah Alper** (1909–1995) with **David Haig** and **Michael Clarke** at Hammersmith Hospital in London (**May 1966** *BBRC* & **20 May 1967** *Nature*) blasted scrapie brain extracts with massive doses of **ionizing radiation** and **`254-nanometre` ultraviolet light** (the exact wavelength absorbed by DNA and RNA `016`).
  - Control viruses (`064`) were wiped out completely — **yet the scrapie agent kept right on infecting mice**! Alper concluded that the agent **did not depend on nucleic acid (`DNA or RNA`) to replicate**, and four months later — on **9 September 1967** (*Nature*) — British biophysicist **John Stanley Griffith** (1928–1972) showed mathematically how a **misfolded host protein** could catalyse its own replication without violating thermodynamics!

- **Ends on:** Biologists treated Alper and Griffith's "protein-only" idea as scientific heresy (`because Francis Crick's 1958 Central Dogma stated that information flows only from DNA and RNA to protein, never from protein to protein!`). Who dared to purify the mystery agent in **San Francisco in 1982** — and what shocking secret did he find when he sequenced its gene in **1985**?

### PART 03: 9 April 1982 & 19 April 1985 — Stanley Prusiner's `Prion` Heresy & the Enemy Inside Our Own Genome — Midpoint Twist (3:10–4:55)
- **Cold open ("Case closed — every infectious disease is an alien invader that brings its own foreign DNA or RNA into your body! Wrong! In 1982 and 1985, scientists proved that the infectious particle has zero nucleic acid — and that the gene that makes it is sitting inside every single healthy human brain right now!"):** **University of California, San Francisco (UCSF), 9 April 1982 & 19 April 1985**.
- **9 April 1982 (*Science*) — Stanley Prusiner isolates `PrP 27–30` and coins `Prion`:**
  - After watching a patient die of **`Creutzfeldt-Jakob disease` (`CJD`)** in 1972, young UCSF neurologist **Stanley B. Prusiner** (born 1942) spent ten years purifying the scrapie agent from thousands of Syrian hamster brains (`which shortened the assay time from 1 year in mice to 60 days`).
  - On **9 April 1982** (*Science*), Prusiner published *"Novel proteinaceous infectious particles cause scrapie"* and coined the word **`prion`** (**`pr`**oteinaceous **`i`**nfectious particle): every chemical or enzyme that digested **DNA or RNA (`nucleases, UV light, psoralens, Zn²⁺ hydrolysis`)** did **zero damage** to infectivity, whereas every treatment that destroyed or unfolded **proteins (`proteinase K, SDS detergent, phenol, chaotropic guanidinium ions`)** destroyed infectivity!
  - At the core of every infectious preparation sat a single protease-resistant protein of `27,000–30,000 daltons` (**`PrP 27–30`**).
- **19 April 1985 (*Cell*) & December 1993 (*PNAS*) — The twist that stunned biology (`The prion gene is in our own DNA!`) & how one protein folds two ways:**
  - If `PrP` was a protein, where was the gene that made it? In **1984–19 April 1985** (*Cell*), **Prusiner**, **Leroy Hood** (Caltech), and **Charles Weissmann** (Zurich) determined the amino-acid sequence of `PrP`, built a DNA probe, and searched for the gene — expecting to find it only in infected animals.
  - Instead, the **`PRNP` gene** lit up inside the chromosomes of **every healthy hamster, mouse, and human on Earth**!
  - Why doesn't our own normal prion protein (**`PrP^C`**) kill us? As **Prusiner, Fred Cohen, and colleagues** proved in **December 1993** (*PNAS*), it's a matter of **3D origami**: normal **`PrP^C`** is folded into **springy `alpha-helices` (`43% alpha-helix, 3% beta-sheet`)** that enzymes easily recycle. The diseased prion (**`PrP^Sc`**) has the **exact same amino-acid sequence**, but is misfolded into flat, rigid, sticky **`beta-sheets` (`43% beta-sheet`)** that stack into indestructible rods (`amyloid fibrils`) and cannot be digested by cellular enzymes or cooked out by boiling!
- *Markers:* `· WHEN: 1972 · WHEN: 9 April 1982 · WHEN: 19 April 1985 · WHEN: December 1993 · FACT: Same protein, two 3D folds · DATA: inked Prusiner (9 April 1982 Science 216: 136–144), Basler, Oesch, Weissmann & Prusiner (19 April 1985 Cell 41: 341–346), and Pan et al. (December 1993 PNAS 90: 10962–10966) Fourier-transform infrared (FTIR) secondary structure & protease-resistance chart on period paper comparing cellular PrPC (43% alpha-helix, 3% beta-sheet, digested by proteinase K) vs infectious PrPSc (30% alpha-helix, 43% beta-sheet, resistant PrP 27–30 core) · SCALE: normal PrPC and deadly PrPSc are built from the exact same string of amino-acid beads — the difference is like folding the exact same sheet of steel into a soft mattress spring versus hammering it into a flat crowbar that bends every spring it touches into another crowbar`
- **Ends on:** Even after 1985, fierce critics insisted Prusiner's protein was just a bystander and that a tiny undetected virus was still hiding in his test tubes. How did **Charles Weissmann** in **Zurich on 2 July 1993** design a single genetic experiment on mice that settled the war forever — right in the middle of Britain's **Mad Cow Disease** crisis?

### PART 04: 2 July 1993 & 1986–1997 — Charles Weissmann's Knockout Mouse & the `Mad Cow` (`BSE`) Crisis (4:55–6:40)
- **Cold open (how do you prove beyond a shadow of a doubt that a disease is spread by a falling protein domino and not by an invisible virus? You breed a mouse with no standing dominoes in its brain — and inject it with the deadliest prions on Earth!):** **Institute of Molecular Biology, University of Zurich, 2 July 1993 & United Kingdom, 1986–1997**.
- **2 July 1993 (*Cell*) — Hansruedi Büeler, Adriano Aguzzi & Charles Weissmann's `Prnp⁰/⁰` knockout mouse:**
  - In **1992–1993** in Zurich, **Charles Weissmann** (born 1931), **Hansruedi Büeler**, and **Adriano Aguzzi** used gene targeting to delete both copies of the normal prion gene (`Prnp`) in mice (**`Prnp⁰/⁰` knockout mice**). Surprisingly, the mice grew up healthy and fertile (`Nature`, 1992).
  - Then came the acid test (**2 July 1993**, *Cell*): Weissmann's team injected lethal doses of mouse-adapted scrapie prions directly into the brains of normal mice (`Prnp+/+`) and prion-knockout mice (`Prnp0/0`).
  - Every normal mouse died of scrapie within **23 weeks (`~158 days`)**. Every single **prion-knockout mouse (`Prnp0/0`) survived past 70+ weeks (`>500 days`) with zero symptoms, zero brain damage, and zero prion multiplication**! And when Weissmann grafted a tiny piece of normal `Prnp+/+` brain tissue into a knockout mouse's brain (1996), prions destroyed the graft while the surrounding `Prnp0/0` brain tissue stayed completely unharmed!
- **1986–1997 — Britain's `Mad Cow Disease` (`BSE`) epidemic & Prusiner's 1997 Nobel Prize:**
  - The proof arrived at a critical moment in history: starting in **November 1986**, over **180,000 British cattle** developed **Bovine Spongiform Encephalopathy (`BSE` / `Mad Cow Disease`)** after cattle were fed meat-and-bone meal containing prion-infected sheep/cattle offal that had survived industrial rendering temperatures!
  - Because prions are misfolded proteins (`beta-sheets`) rather than living bacteria or viruses, **cooking beef at normal temperatures could not destroy them** — and in **March–April 1996** (*The Lancet*), British neurologists **Robert Will** and **James Ironside** identified **variant Creutzfeldt-Jakob disease (`vCJD`)** in young human patients (`proven by John Collinge and Moira Bruce in October 1996–1997 Nature to carry the exact same prion glycosylation/strain signature as BSE cattle`). Feed bans stopped the epidemic in its tracks, and in **October 1997**, **Stanley Prusiner** was awarded the sole **Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine**!
- *Markers:* `· WHO: Adriano Aguzzi (born 1960) — Italian-Swiss neuropathologist · WHEN: November 1986 · WHEN: 1992 · WHEN: 2 July 1993 · WHEN: April 1996 · WHEN: October 1997 · FACT: Knockout mice 100% immune · DATA: inked Büeler & Weissmann (2 July 1993 Cell 73: 1339–1347) brain prion-titre curve (log LD50 units over 50 weeks in Prnp+/+, Prnp+/0, and Prnp0/0 mice) alongside the UK BSE epidemic curve (1986–2000, peaking in 1992–1993 after the July 1988 ruminant feed ban) on period paper · SCALE: a normal mouse brain is a room packed with standing PrPC dominoes where one PrPSc spark triggers a fatal avalanche in twenty-three weeks, whereas a Prnp0/0 knockout mouse brain is an empty floor where the spark simply dies out`
- **Ends on:** Prions proved what happens when a single protein folds into the **wrong** 3D shape. But how do **properly folded proteins (`enzymes`)** act as precision molecular locks that speed up the chemical reactions of life **a billion times** — and how did **crushed brewer's yeast with zero living cells in Munich in 1897** shatter Louis Pasteur's "vital force" myth and birth modern biochemistry?
