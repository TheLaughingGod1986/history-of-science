### PART 02: October 1667, 1904 & 1910 — Hooke and Lower's Scarlet Blood, Christian Bohr's Curve & the Kroghs' "Seven Little Devils" (1:25–3:10)
- **Cold open (why for fifteen hundred years after Galen, doctors believed the lungs were just radiator fans to cool a literal fire burning inside the heart — until two Englishmen pumped fireplace bellows into a lung in October 1667, and a Danish husband-and-wife team in 1910 overturned their own mentor's dogma with seven back-to-back papers nicknamed "The Seven Little Devils"!):** **Royal Society, London, October 1667 & University of Copenhagen, Denmark, 1904–1910**.
- **24 October 1667 (*Phil. Trans. R. Soc.*) & 1669 (*Tractatus de Corde*) — Robert Hooke & Richard Lower prove blood turns scarlet in the lungs:**
  - After **William Harvey (`005`)** proved in 1628 that blood circulates in a closed loop, nobody knew *why* blood went through the lungs. On **24 October 1667** at the Royal Society, **Robert Hooke** (1635–1703) connected a pair of continuous bellows to keep a lung inflated with fresh air without any chest movement, and physician **Richard Lower** (1631–1691, in his **1669** *Tractatus de Corde*) proved that dark purple venous blood turns **bright scarlet right inside the lungs** simply by touching fresh air (`which Antoine Lavoisier 017 identified as oxygen in 1777, and Marcello Malpighi had seen passing through microscopic lung capillaries and air vesicles in 1661`)!
- **1904 & 1910 (*Skandinavisches Archiv für Physiologie*) — Christian Bohr vs August & Marie Krogh ("The Seven Little Devils"):**
  - In **1904** in Copenhagen, **Christian Bohr** (1855–1911 — father of atomic physicist Niels Bohr `004` and grandfather of Aage Bohr!), working with **Karl Hasselbalch** and his young student **August Krogh** (1874–1949), discovered the **`Bohr Effect`**: when muscles work hard and release **carbon dioxide (`CO₂`) and acid**, they change the shape of blood's oxygen-carrying protein (**`haemoglobin`**) and force it to **dump oxygen right where the muscles need it most**!
  - Yet Christian Bohr (and Oxford's **John Scott Haldane**) clung to one giant mistake: they insisted that at high altitude or during exercise, living cells in the lung walls actively **pumped (`secreted`)** oxygen into the blood against pressure!
  - To test his beloved teacher's theory, **August Krogh** and his wife, physician **Marie Krogh** (1874–1943), invented a microscopic gas-bubble analyser (**`the microtonometer`**) that measured the exact oxygen pressure of a tiny air bubble equilibrated with blood.
  - In **1910**, the Kroghs published **seven back-to-back papers** in *Skandinavisches Archiv für Physiologie* — famous in physiology as **"The Seven Little Devils"** — proving with devastating precision that oxygen pressure in the lung air sacs (`alveoli`) is **always higher** than in the blood! The lungs never pump a single molecule of oxygen: across **300 million `alveoli`** (`a 70-to-100-square-metre tennis court just 0.5 micrometres thick`), oxygen flows into your blood by **100% passive physical diffusion** (`winning August Krogh the 1920 Nobel Prize`)!

### PART 03: 1937 – 13 February 1960 — Max Perutz's 22-Year X-Ray Quest & the Molecular Lung (`Haemoglobin`) — Midpoint Twist (3:10–4:55)
- **Cold open ("Case closed — haemoglobin is just a passive iron bucket that carries four oxygen molecules the way a shopping bag carries four apples! Wrong! - **Why haemoglobin's S-curve baffled chemists (`Felix Hoppe-Seyler 1862`, `Christian Bohr 1904`, `Gilbert Adair 1925`):**
  - Ever since **Felix Hoppe-Seyler** named and crystallised **`haemoglobin`** in **1862–1864** and **Gilbert Adair (1925)** proved each haemoglobin molecule carries **four iron (`Fe²⁺`) `haem` groups** (`so each red blood cell with 270 million haemoglobin molecules carries over 1 billion oxygen molecules!`), scientists were puzzled by **Christian Bohr's 1904 S-shaped (`sigmoidal`) oxygen curve**.
  - Unlike single-subunit muscle **`myoglobin`** (whose structure **John Kendrew** solved in **1958**), haemoglobin refuses to let go of oxygen in the lungs, yet dumps it rapidly in hungry tissues — because binding the **first** oxygen molecule somehow makes it **hundreds of times easier** for the second, third, and fourth oxygens to jump aboard (**`cooperative binding`**)! How could four iron atoms sitting far apart in the same protein talk to each other?
- **1937 – 13 February 1960 (*Nature*) — Max Perutz solves the phase problem (`1953`) and unveils the 3D breathing machine of `Haemoglobin`:**
  - Starting in **1937** at the Cavendish Laboratory in Cambridge (`under Lawrence Bragg 003, alongside Francis Crick and James Watson 016`), Austrian-born chemist **Max Perutz** (1914–2002) spent **22 years** trying to decode the X-ray diffraction spots of horse haemoglobin crystals — a molecule of **10,000 atoms**!
  - In **1953**, Perutz cracked the seemingly impossible X-ray **"phase problem"** by soaking haemoglobin crystals in heavy **mercury (`Hg`) atoms** (`isomorphous replacement`, comparing the X-ray spots with and without mercury!).
  - On **13 February 1960** (*Nature*, winning **Perutz and John Kendrew the 1962 Nobel Prize in Chemistry**), Perutz unveiled the 3D atomic model of **`haemoglobin`** (and its full `T`-to-`R` mechanism in **1970**): when the **first oxygen molecule** binds to one iron atom, it pulls the iron atom just **`0.04 nanometres` (`0.4 angstroms`)** into the flat haem ring, tugging the attached histidine lever and **rotating the four protein subunits by 15 degrees** from the tight **`T (Tense)` state** into the open **`R (Relaxed)` state**!

### PART 04: 1929, 1957 & May 1959 — Kurt von Neergaard, John Clements & Mary Ellen Avery's Discovery of `Pulmonary Surfactant` (4:55–6:40)
- **Cold open (in 1948, Harvard Medical School refused to admit Mary Ellen Avery because she was a woman — eleven years later, in May 1959 in Boston, she discovered why tens of thousands of premature babies were suffocating after birth, and unlocked a therapy that has saved millions of newborn lives!):** **University of Basel, 1929 & Harvard School of Public Health / Boston Lying-in Hospital, May 1959**.
- **1929 (*Z. Gesamte Exp. Med.*) & 1957 — Kurt von Neergaard's salt-water lung & John Clements's Langmuir trough:**
  - By **Laplace's Law (`P = 2T / r`)**, the inward collapsing pressure (`P`) of a wet bubble equals twice the surface tension (`T`) divided by the bubble's radius (`r`). That means as you exhale and your `300 million alveoli` shrink to a tiny radius, water's fierce surface tension (`72 dynes/cm`) should make the smallest air sacs snap shut and empty into larger ones (`atelectasis`)!
  - In **1929** in Basel, Swiss physician **Kurt von Neergaard** (1887–1947) inflated a pig lung first with air, and then with salt water (`which eliminates the air-water surface inside the lung`): filling the lung with water required **less than a third of the pressure** needed to inflate it with air! Most of the work of breathing isn't stretching rubbery lung tissue — it is fighting **water surface tension**!
  - Between **1955 and 1957**, British physicist **Richard Pattle** (studying nerve-gas lung foam) and American physiologist **John A. Clements** (1923–2024, using an improvised **Langmuir surface balance** `071`) discovered that healthy lungs secrete a magical phospholipid-protein soap (**`pulmonary surfactant`**, rich in `dipalmitoylphosphatidylcholine, DPPC`): as an alveolus shrinks during exhalation, the surfactant molecules pack together and slash surface tension to **nearly zero (`<5 dynes/cm`)**, stopping the air sacs from collapsing!
- **May 1959 (*AMA Journal of Diseases of Children*) — Mary Ellen Avery & Jere Mead solve "Hyaline Membrane Disease" (`Respiratory Distress Syndrome`):**
  - After being rejected by Harvard Medical School because their quota of women was full, **Mary Ellen Avery** (1927–2011) earned her MD at Johns Hopkins, survived tuberculosis herself (`spending two years studying lung physiology in bed!`), and joined **Jere Mead** (1920–2009) in Boston.
  - At the time, **25,000 premature babies a year in the United States** (including President John F. Kennedy's newborn son Patrick in August 1963) died within days of birth from **"Hyaline Membrane Disease" (`Neonatal Respiratory Distress Syndrome`)** — gasping for air as their lungs turned stiff and airless. Doctors thought a sticky membrane was *blocking* their airways.
  - Avery visited John Clements's lab, returned to Boston, and tested lung extracts from 9 premature babies who had died of the disease on a Langmuir trough. Published in **May 1959** (*AMA J. Dis. Child.*), **Avery and Mead** proved the shocking truth: nothing was blocking the airways — premature babies' lungs collapsed on every breath (`with surface tension stuck at 30 dynes/cm instead of dropping below 5 dynes/cm`) because the fetal lung doesn't start making **`pulmonary surfactant`** until the final weeks of pregnancy!
  - Her discovery led directly to **Mont Liggins and Ross Howie's 1972** prenatal steroid injections (`to speed up fetal surfactant before premature birth`) and **Tetsuro Fujiwara's January 1980** (*Lancet*) natural surfactant replacement therapy — cutting newborn respiratory deaths by over 90% and winning Mary Ellen Avery the **National Medal of Science** (and the seat of Physician-in-Chief at Boston Children's Hospital and Professor at the very Harvard Medical School that had rejected her)!
