### PART 02: July 1901 – 15 February 1902 — The Royal Yacht *Princesse Alice II*, the Dog Named Neptune & Why Vaccination Went Backwards (`Anaphylaxis`) (1:25–3:05)
- **Cold open (why every scientist in 1901 believed a small dose of poison always makes you stronger):** **Aboard the Yacht *Princesse Alice II*, Cape Verde Islands, July 1901 & Faculty of Medicine, Paris, 15 February 1902**. After **Edward Jenner (`007`)** defeated smallpox, **Louis Pasteur (`001`)** conquered rabies, and **Emil von Behring** created diphtheria antitoxin in `1890`, medicine had an unbreakable law: exposing an animal to small doses of a germ or toxin builds **protection (`prophylaxis`)**.
- **July 1901 — Prince Albert I of Monaco's jellyfish problem:** In **July 1901**, oceanographer **Prince Albert I of Monaco** invited Parisian physiologist **Charles Richet** (1850–1935) and marine biologist **Paul Portier** (1866–1962) aboard his research yacht ***Princesse Alice II*** in the tropical Atlantic and asked them to study the painful, paralysing venom of the **Portuguese man-of-war** (*Physalia*) and **sea anemones** (*Actinia*).
- **15 February 1902 — The dog named Neptune & the birth of *Anaphylaxis* (`Société de Biologie`):**
  - Back in Paris, Richet and Portier extracted toxin from sea-anemone tentacles (`actinocongestin`) to immunise dogs so they could make a protective antivenom.
  - When they injected a dog named **Neptune** with a tiny, non-lethal dose, Neptune barely noticed. Within days he was barking, eating, and completely healthy.
  - **Twenty-two days later, on 15 February 1902**, Richet and Portier injected Neptune with a tiny second booster dose — just **`0.10 cc per kilogram` (`less than one-twentieth of the dose needed to harm an untreated dog`)**, expecting Neptune to shrug it off completely.
  - Instead, within **seconds**, Neptune began gasping, collapsed, and died in **25 minutes**!
  - Stunned, Richet and Portier repeated the test across dozens of animals and proved a brand-new biological law: when a foreign protein enters the bloodstream, the first dose doesn't protect — after a **2-to-3-week latent incubation period**, it turns the immune system into a hair-trigger bomb where a tiny second dose is catastrophic!
  - Richet coined the word ***anaphylaxis*** (`from Greek ana- "against" + phylaxis "protection"`, winning the **1913 Nobel Prize**), and on **24 July 1906**, Viennese paediatrician **Clemens von Pirquet** (1874–1929) coined the word ***allergy*** (`from Greek allos "other" + ergon "reaction"`) to describe how the immune system's reactivity had been altered!
- *Markers:* `· WHEN: July 1901 · WHEN: 15 February 1902 · WHEN: 24 July 1906 · WHEN: 1913 · FACT: "Against protection" (1902) · DATA: inked dose-response & latent-period chart on period paper from Portier & Richet (15 February 1902, C. R. Soc. Biol. 54: 170–172) contrasting an untreated dog surviving >2.0 cc/kg of actinia toxin for days against a primed dog (22 days after a tiny first dose) suffering fatal shock within 25 minutes from just 0.10 cc/kg (a >20-fold drop in lethal threshold) · SCALE: a dose twenty times too small to hurt a first-time animal became lethal in minutes on the second visit — because the danger wasn't the jellyfish poison anymore, it was the body's own counter-attack`
- **Ends on:** Richet proved that the tiny second dose couldn't possibly be enough poison to kill the animal directly — the animal's **own body** had to be manufacturing and releasing a violent internal chemical poison! What was that chemical bomb hiding inside our own tissues?

### PART 03: 1910, 1937 & 1953 — Henry Dale's Histamine Bomb, Mast Cells & Daniel Bovet's First Antihistamine — Midpoint Twist (3:05–4:50)
- **Cold open ("Case closed? Wrong — the poison in an allergic attack isn't in the peanut or the pollen at all; it's pre-packed inside your own cells like microscopic hand grenades!"):** **Wellcome Physiological Research Laboratories, London, 1910 & Pasteur Institute, Paris, 1937**.
- **1910–1927 — Henry Hallett Dale isolates Histamine (`β-imidazolylethylamine`) & proves it mimics anaphylactic shock:**
  - In **1910** in London, British pharmacologist **Henry Hallett Dale** (1875–1968; `who also proved chemical acetylcholine transmission with Otto Loewi in 033!`) and chemist **George Barger** isolated a potent nitrogen compound from rye ergot fungus called **histamine** (`β-imidazolylethylamine`), and in **1927** (`Best, Dale, Dudley & Thorpe`) proved that histamine is naturally stored inside **normal healthy animal lungs and liver**!
  - When Dale and **Patrick Playfair Laidlaw** (**1910–1919**) injected pure **histamine** into a healthy animal that had never seen a foreign protein in its life, the animal suffered **the exact same symptoms as Richet's anaphylactic shock**: airway smooth muscles clamped shut (`bronchospasm / asthma`), tiny blood vessels dilated and leaked fluid into the skin (`hives and swelling`), and blood pressure plunged (`while an injection of adrenaline / epinephrine — isolated by Jokichi Takamine in 1900–1901 — reversed it by constricting blood vessels and relaxing airways, the basis of today's EpiPen!`)!
- **1953 — James Riley & Geoffrey West find the grenade lockers (`Mast Cells`):** Where was the body hiding all that histamine? In **1953** in Dundee, Scotland, **James F. Riley** and **Geoffrey B. West** proved that histamine is packed inside the dark purple granules of **mast cells** (`first spotted and named Mastzellen by Paul Ehrlich in 1877`), stationed like border guards under your skin, nose, lungs, and gut!
- **1937 — Daniel Bovet & Anne-Marie Staub invent the first Antihistamine (`Pasteur Institute`):**
  - If histamine is the chemical key that unlocks swelling, itching, and shock, could a chemist build a **dummy key** to jam the lock?
  - At the **Pasteur Institute in Paris in 1937**, Swiss-Italian pharmacologist **Daniel Bovet** (1907–1992) and **Anne-Marie Staub** screened synthetic phenolic ethers and discovered **`929F` (`thymoxyethyldiethylamine`)** — the world's **first antihistamine**, which blocked histamine receptors and protected guinea pigs from lethal anaphylactic shock (`leading to human antihistamines like phenbenzamine / Antergan in 1942 and diphenhydramine / Benadryl in 1946, and earning Bovet the 1957 Nobel Prize`)!

- **Ends on:** Dale, Riley, and Bovet explained the *explosive* (`histamine` inside `mast cells`). But what was the **trigger**? How does a mast cell know the difference between a speck of dust and the specific **cooked fish, cat hair, orragweed pollen** that *you* are allergic to?

### PART 04: 1921 & July 1966 — Prausnitz & Küstner's Fish-Allergy Blood Swap & Kimishige and Teruko Ishizaka's 1-in-10-Million `IgE` Discovery (4:50–6:40)
- **Cold open (how a scientist who was violently allergic to cooked fish proved allergies travel in a drop of blood — and why it took 45 years to find the invisible culprit):** **Breslau, Germany, 1921 & Children's Asthma Research Institute, Denver, Colorado, July 1966**.
- **1921 — Carl Prausnitz & Heinz Küstner's daring self-experiment (`The P-K Reaction`):**
  - German bacteriologist **Heinz Küstner** (1897–1963) suffered violent hives, asthma, and vomiting whenever he ate a tiny bite of **cooked fish**.
  - In **1921**, his colleague **Carl Prausnitz** (1876–1963, who suffered from hay fever but could eat fish all day!) drew a tiny drop of **clear blood serum (`cell-free`)** from Küstner's arm and injected it under the skin of his **own forearm**.
  - The next day, Prausnitz injected a trace of **cooked fish extract** into that exact spot on his arm — and within minutes, **Prausnitz's own skin erupted into a blazing red allergic wheal-and-flare hive**!
  - A mystery substance in the blood (`which they called "reagin"`) had transferred Küstner's fish allergy to Prausnitz's skin! Yet for **45 years (`1921–1966`)**, no laboratory on Earth could find it, because tests for known antibodies (`IgG, IgM, IgA, IgD`) came back completely blank!
- **July 1966 — Kimishige and Teruko Ishizaka find the `1-in-10-million` needle in the haystack (`IgE`, *Journal of Immunology*):**
  - At the **Children's Asthma Research Institute and Hospital in Denver, Colorado**, husband-and-wife immunologists **Kimishige Ishizaka** (1925–2018) and **Teruko Ishizaka** (1926–2019) spent six years purifying fractions of blood serum from ragweed-allergic patients and immunising rabbits to raise ultra-sensitive antiserum against the mystery factor.
  - In **July 1966** (*Journal of Immunology*; confirmed independently via a rare myeloma protein by **S. G. O. Johansson and Hans Bennich** in Uppsala in **1967**, and officially named **`IgE`** by the WHO in **February 1968**), the Ishizakas proved why nobody had ever seen it:
    - It was a brand-new **fifth class of human antibody — Immunoglobulin E (`IgE`)**!
    - While normal germ-fighting **`IgG`** circulates at **`~10,000,000 nanograms per millilitre` (`10 mg/mL`)**, **`IgE`** circulates in healthy blood at just **`~100 nanograms per millilitre` (`0.0001 mg/mL` — 100,000 times rarer, or about 1 part in 10 million of total blood proteins!)** because almost all of it is tightly bolted onto the surface of **mast cells and basophils**!
- **The evolutionary twist — Why did nature invent `IgE` and mast-cell bombs (`to blast out giant parasitic worms!`)?**
  - Why would evolution give every mammal on Earth an `IgE` bomb that can kill us over a peanut?
  - Because for **millions of years**, our ancestors were constantly invaded by **multicellular parasites (`hookworms, roundworms, tapeworms, and blood-sucking ticks`)** that are **millions of times too big** for a single white blood cell to swallow!
  - To fight a giant worm, **`IgE` antibodies** recognise worm proteins and detonate **mast cells**: histamine floods the gut and airways with mucus, triggers violent sneezing, coughing, itching, and muscle contractions (`literally flushing and scratching the worm or tick off your body!`), while recruiting eosinophils to attack the parasite (`and as Ruslan Medzhitov and Stephen Galli showed in 2006–2013, mast-cell enzymes also chew up real snake and bee venoms — meaning Richet's 1901 venom experiment actually triggered a rapid detox defense pushed too far!`)!
  - In our clean modern cities (`as David Strachan proposed in 1989`), with far fewer parasitic worms to fight, an idle `IgE` system mistakes harmless proteins in **pollen, dust mites, or peanuts** for a worm attack!

- **Ends on:** Once we understood *why* the `IgE` system misfires on peanuts and pollen, how did doctors discover that avoiding peanuts in babyhood was actually **making peanut allergies worse** (`Gideon Lack's 2015 LEAP trial — cutting peanut allergy by 81%`)?

Then on **26 February 2015** (*New England Journal of Medicine*, the **LEAP trial**), **Gideon Lack** and colleagues proved that when high-risk infants eat peanut protein early (`starting at 4–11 months`), their gut immune system trains **regulatory T-cells and blocking `IgG4` antibodies** instead of `IgE` — **slashing peanut allergies by `81%`** (`while anti-IgE antibodies like omalizumab and epinephrine auto-injectors protect millions today`)!
- **The 114-year arc:** From Richet and Portier's yacht in the Atlantic (`1901–1902`), to Dale's histamine (`1910`) and Bovet's antihistamine (`1937`), to Kimishige and Teruko Ishizaka's `1-in-10-million` `IgE` discovery in Denver (`1966`), science unmasked the ancient guardian behind every spring sneeze.
