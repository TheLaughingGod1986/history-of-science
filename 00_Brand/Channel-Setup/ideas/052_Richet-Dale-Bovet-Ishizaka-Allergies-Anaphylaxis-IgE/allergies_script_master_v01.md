After Edward Jenner and Louis Pasteur conquered smallpox and rabies, every doctor in **nineteen oh-one** believed an unbreakable rule: giving an animal small doses of a germ or poison always builds **protection**.

In **July nineteen oh-one**, oceanographer **Prince Albert the First of Monaco** invited Parisian physiologist **Charles Richet** and marine biologist **Paul Portier** aboard his research yacht *Princesse Alice the Second* in the Atlantic to study the stinging venom of **Portuguese man-of-war** jellyfish and **sea anemones**.

Back in Paris, Richet and Portier injected a cheerful dog named **Neptune** with a tiny, harmless drop of sea-anemone extract to make him immune. Neptune barely noticed — for **twenty-two days** he ran around barking and healthy! Then, on the **fifteenth of February nineteen oh-two**, they gave Neptune a tiny second booster dose — just **one-tenth of a millilitre per kilogram**, less than **one-twentieth** of the amount needed to harm an untreated dog!

Instead of being protected, Neptune collapsed in shock within minutes! Repeating the test, Richet proved that after a **two-to-three-week incubation window**, the first dose had wired the immune system **backwards** into extreme sensitivity! He named it **anaphylaxis** — Greek for **"against protection"**, winning the **nineteen thirteen** Nobel Prize — and on the **twenty-fourth of July nineteen oh-six**, Viennese doctor **Clemens von Pirquet** coined the word **allergy**!

That tiny second dose was far too small to poison the body directly. So what explosive chemical was the body's own cells setting off?

In **nineteen ten** in London, British pharmacologist **Henry Hallett Dale** studied a nitrogen molecule called **histamine** — and proved in **nineteen twenty-seven** that our own healthy lungs and skin are loaded with it! When Dale injected pure histamine into a healthy animal that had never been sensitised to anything, it suffered **instant anaphylactic shock**: airways clamped shut, blood vessels leaked fluid into hives, and blood pressure plunged — until a shot of **adrenaline** reopened the airways and tightened the blood vessels, the exact lifesaver inside a modern **EpiPen**!

In **nineteen twenty-one**, German scientist **Heinz Küstner** — who broke out in violent hives whenever he ate **cooked fish** — let his colleague **Carl Prausnitz** inject a tiny drop of Küstner's clear blood serum into Prausnitz's own arm. The next day, when Prausnitz touched cooked fish extract to that spot on his skin, **Prausnitz's arm erupted into a blazing fish-allergy hive**!

Something in the blood carried the allergy — yet for forty-five years, every test for known antibodies came back blank! Then, in **July nineteen sixty-six** in Denver, husband-and-wife immunologists **Kimishige and Teruko Ishizaka** — confirmed in **nineteen sixty-seven** and officially named in **February nineteen sixty-eight** — finally isolated the culprit: a brand-new fifth class of human antibody called **Immunoglobulin E, or `IgE`**!

Nobody had found it before because normal **`IgG` antibodies** float in your blood at **ten million nanograms per millilitre**, whereas **`IgE`** is present at just **one hundred nanograms per millilitre — one hundred thousand times rarer**, like one red marble hidden in ten million white ones!

And why did evolution give us `IgE`? Because a single white blood cell trying to swallow a **parasitic hookworm or tick** is like a mouse trying to swallow a python! Evolution built the **`IgE` mast-cell bomb** to flood your gut and airways with mucus, sneezing, and itching to blast giant worms out and chew up real venoms — and in clean modern cities with no worms to fight, that idle tripwire misfires on **pollen and peanuts**!

Once we knew how `IgE` works, why did a **twenty fifteen** study prove that keeping babies *away* from peanuts was actually **causing** peanut allergies?

Because an allergy is an **over-armed** `IgE` defense — and when a baby only meets peanut dust through dry skin instead of food, the skin sounds the `IgE` alarm! On the **twenty-sixth of February twenty fifteen**, paediatric allergist **Gideon Lack** proved in the landmark **LEAP trial** that feeding babies smooth peanut protein early in infancy trains the gut's peacekeeper cells instead of `IgE`, **slashing peanut allergies by eighty-one per cent**!
