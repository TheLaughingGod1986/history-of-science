### PART 02: March 1894 – 1901 — George Oliver, Edward Schäfer & Jokichi Takamine Crystallise `Adrenaline` (1:25–3:05)
- **Cold open (why anatomists for over 300 years stared at the tiny triangular glands sitting like Napoleon hats on top of your kidneys — the adrenal glands — and had no idea what they were for):** **University College London, March 1894 & New York City, July 1900 – 1901**.
- **1855 & March 1894 — Thomas Addison's mystery disease & George Oliver and Edward Schäfer's mercury column shock:**
  - In **1855**, London physician **Thomas Addison** (1793–1860) showed that patients whose **adrenal glands (`suprarenal capsules`)** were destroyed by tuberculosis wasted away and died (`Addison's disease`), while in **1856–1889** French physiologist **Charles-Édouard Brown-Séquard** (1817–1894) suggested that ductless glands pour "internal secretions" into the blood.
  - In **March 1894**, physician **George Oliver** (1841–1915) — who had invented a spring gauge to measure the diameter of his own son's wrist artery after feeding him extracts of sheep adrenal glands — visited Professor **Edward Sharpey-Schäfer** (1850–1935) at University College London.
  - Sceptical at first, Schäfer injected a few drops of Oliver's adrenal extract into a vein on **March 1894** — and stood amazed as the **mercury float in his blood-pressure manometer shot almost out the top of the glass tube**!
- **July–November 1900 & 1901 — Jokichi Takamine & Keizo Uenaka crystallise the first pure hormone (`Adrenaline`):**
  - After **John Jacob Abel** (1857–1938) at Johns Hopkins isolated an impure benzoate derivative he called *"epinephrine"* in **1897–1899**, Japanese chemist **Jokichi Takamine** (1854–1922) and his 24-year-old assistant **Keizo Uenaka** (1876–1960) working in a **New York City laboratory on 21 July – November 1900** (`patented in 1901`) succeeded in precipitating bundles of **pure, microscopic needle crystals (`C9H13NO3`)** that raised blood pressure at **one part in a million** — naming the world's first isolated hormone **`Adrenalin`**!

- **Ends on:** Adrenaline proved a gland could dump a powerful chemical into the blood during an emergency. Yet for everyday digestion and organ control, the great Russian physiologist **Ivan Pavlov** insisted the **nervous system (`nervism`)** did all the talking. What happened on the afternoon of **16 January 1902** when two brothers-in-law in London tested Pavlov's rule — and cut every nerve?

### PART 03: 16 January 1902 — William Bayliss & Ernest Starling Cut Every Nerve and Discover `Secretin` — Midpoint Twist (3:05–4:50)
- **Cold open ("Case closed — when food leaves your stomach, electrical nerve wires tell your pancreas to pour out digestive juice! Wrong! Even when you cut every single nerve in the gut, the pancreas still fires right on schedule!"):** **University College London, afternoon of Thursday, 16 January 1902**.
- **The dogma of Ivan Pavlov (`Nervism`):**
  - Whenever acidic food (`chyme`, `032`) squirts out of your stomach into the upper small intestine (`the duodenum and jejunum`), your **pancreas** immediately pours out alkaline pancreatic juice to neutralise the acid and digest the meal.
  - In St. Petersburg, **Ivan Pavlov** (1849–1936; `1904 Nobel Prize`) and his student Popielski taught that acid in the gut triggers **sensory nerve endings** that telegraph the brain (`via the vagus and splanchnic nerves`) to command the pancreas to fire.
- **Thursday, 16 January 1902 — The decisive afternoon at University College London (*J. Physiol.* 1902):**
  - On the afternoon of **16 January 1902**, British physiologists and brothers-in-law **William Maddock Bayliss** (1860–1924) and **Ernest Henry Starling** (1866–1927) decided to test how that local nerve reflex worked in an anaesthetised dog.
  - They cut the **vagus nerves**, removed the **solar plexus (`coeliac ganglion`)**, and meticulously **severed every single nerve filament** around a loop of upper intestine (`jejunum`), leaving the intestinal loop connected to the body **only by its arteries and veins**.
  - When they injected a few drops of **`0.4%` hydrochloric acid** into the completely denervated intestinal loop, **after two minutes the pancreas began pouring out digestive juice just as fast as before** (`and bystander Charles J. Martin remarked: "What a great afternoon!"`)!
- **The mortar-and-sand proof (`Secretin`):**
  - Starling immediately exclaimed: *"Then it must be a chemical reflex!"* Why couldn't it be the acid itself travelling in the blood? Because injecting `0.4%` acid directly into a vein did **nothing**!
  - Right there at the bench on **16 January 1902**, Bayliss and Starling cut out a piece of intestinal mucous lining, **ground it in a mortar with sand and dilute hydrochloric acid**, filtered the liquid, and **injected it into the jugular vein** — and within seconds, the pancreas gushed out a torrent of juice!
  - Acid doesn't trigger the pancreas directly; acid causes the cells lining the gut to release a **chemical messenger — which they named `secretin` — into the bloodstream**!
- *Markers:* `· WHO: Ivan P. Pavlov (1849–1936) — Russian physiologist · WHEN: 16 January 1902 · FACT: Cut every nerve; gut still talked · DATA: inked Bayliss & Starling (1902, Proc. R. Soc. Lond. 69: 352–353 & J. Physiol. 28: 325–353) four-step experimental proof chart on period paper comparing (1) acid in intact duodenum -> secretion, (2) acid in completely denervated jejunum loop -> full secretion after ~2 min, (3) acid injected directly into blood vein -> 0 secretion, and (4) acid-ground jejunal mucosa extract (`secretin`) injected into vein -> massive pancreatic secretion · SCALE: when Pavlov's lab in St. Petersburg repeated Bayliss and Starling's nerve-cut experiment and saw the pancreas pour out juice with every wire cut, Pavlov stepped out of his office, shook his head, and said: "Of course they are right — we cannot claim a monopoly on truth"`
- **Ends on:** Bayliss and Starling had found a brand-new communication system in the body — **chemical messengers travelling by blood**. What should science call these messengers, and how did a dinner in Cambridge in **1905** give us the word **"hormone"**?

### PART 04: 20 June 1905 – 1977 — Coining the Word "Hormone" & How the Brain Commands Every Gland (`The Master Switch`) (4:50–6:40)
- **Cold open (how a conversation over dinner at a Cambridge college in 1905 gave science the Greek word for the chemical letters running your growth, metabolism, stress, and blood sugar):** **Gonville and Caius College, Cambridge & Royal College of Physicians, London, 20 June 1905**.
- **20 June 1905 — William Hardy, W. T. Vesey & Ernest Starling coin the word "Hormone":**
  - Needed a single word for chemical substances produced in one organ and carried by the bloodstream to excite another organ, **Ernest Starling** asked biologist **William Bate Hardy** (1864–1934) at dinner at Gonville and Caius College, Cambridge.
  - Together with classical scholar **William Trevor Vesey** (Lendrum), they chose the Greek verb *hormaō* / participle **`hormōn`** (**"I excite"** or **"set in motion"**) — and Starling announced the word **"hormone"** to the world in his Croonian Lecture on **20 June 1905** (*The Lancet*)!
- **The explosion of endocrinology (`1914–1977` — Thyroxine, Insulin, Cortisone, & the Hypothalamus-Pituitary Master Link):**
  - Once scientists knew to look for blood-borne chemical messengers (`hormones`), one medical mystery after another fell:
    - **Christmas Day, 25 December 1914:** **Edward C. Kendall** (1886–1972) at the Mayo Clinic crystallised the thyroid hormone (**`thyroxine`**), which sets the metabolic speed of every cell in your body (`and in 1936–1948 isolated adrenal cortisone, winning the 1950 Nobel Prize with Tadeus Reichstein and Philip Hench`)!
    - **1921–1922:** Inspired directly by Starling's hormone lectures, **Frederick Banting, Charles Best, and James Collip (`035`)** isolated **`insulin`** from the pancreas, saving millions of diabetic lives!
    - **1955–1977:** And how do the **nervous system (`033`)** and the **hormone system (`060`)** connect? British anatomist **Geoffrey Harris** (1913–1971) in **1955** and Nobel laureates **Roger Guillemin** (1924–2024) and **Andrew Schally** (1926–2024) in **1969–1971** (alongside **Rosalyn Yalow's 1959** radioimmunoassay that measures hormones down to a trillionth of a gram, `1977 Nobel Prize`) proved that the base of your brain (**`the hypothalamus`**) sends microscopic hormone pulses down a tiny blood bridge to the pea-sized **`pituitary gland`**, uniting nerves and hormones into one master network!

### PART 05: Why Your Nerves and Hormones Are Two Halves of One System — & How Your Cells Tell Time in Pitch Darkness (6:40–8:15)
- **Cold open (the misconception that nerves and hormones are rivals — when your brain is actually the largest hormone gland in your body, translating electrical thoughts into chemical surges every second):** Why did **Ivan Pavlov** and **Ernest Starling** initially clash over whether nerves or chemicals run the body? Because your body needs **both**: electrical nerves (`033`) are like **high-speed fibre-optic cables** that fire a single muscle in milliseconds, while hormones (`060`) are like **wireless radio broadcasts** through your blood (`005`) that coordinate trillions of cells across hours, days, and decades!
- **After-act hand-off (7:45–8:15):**
  - Every morning before you even open your eyes, your adrenal glands release a surge of **cortisol (`060`)** and your body temperature rises to prepare you for the day.
  - How do your glands and cells know **what time it is**?
  - In **1729**, French astronomer **Jean-Jacques d'Ortous de Mairan** locked a sensitive plant inside a **pitch-black cupboard** in Paris — and discovered its leaves still opened at sunrise and folded at sunset without ever seeing the Sun!
  - How did **Jürgen Aschoff's 1962 underground bunker volunteers**, **Seymour Benzer and Ronald Konopka's September 1971 mutant fruit flies**, and **Jeffrey Hall, Michael Rosbash, and Michael Young (`1984–1994`, 2017 Nobel Prize)** prove that **almost every single cell in your body carries its own self-winding 24-hour molecular clock** (`061` candidate *How Does Every Cell in Your Body Tell Time in Pitch Darkness (`The Circadian Clock & Period Gene`)?*)?
