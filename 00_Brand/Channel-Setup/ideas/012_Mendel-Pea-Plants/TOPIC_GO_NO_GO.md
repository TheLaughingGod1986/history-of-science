## 2. Topic Fit & Packaging (`HOS_STRATEGY.md` & `THUMBNAIL_AND_TITLE_RULES.md`)

- **The lane:** *A familiar thing, and the moment we found out the truth about it.*
  - **Familiar thing:** Why a child can have their grandfather's red hair or grandmother's green eyes when neither parent has them.
  - **Person & room:** Augustinian friar **Gregor Johann Mendel** (1822–1884), who failed his Vienna teaching certificate twice, working in the 35×7 m walled garden and warm candlelit study of **St Thomas's Abbey in Brünn** (1856–1863).
  - **Moment of proof:** Hand-pollinating ~28,000 pea plants with forceps, a camel-hair paintbrush and calico bags; counting 7,324 seeds in one trial (5,474 smooth : 1,850 wrinkled = **2.96 : 1**) and 8,023 for colour (6,022 yellow : 2,001 green = **3.01 : 1**) to prove traits travel as discrete dominant and recessive pairs rather than blending like paint.
  - **Midpoint twist (~60%):** Nobody at his February/March 1865 Brünn lectures understood him; Munich botanist **Carl von Nägeli** steered him into testing **hawkweed (*Hieracium*)**, which reproduces asexually by apomixis (discovered 1903), making Mendel's own 3:1 law appear to fail. His papers were burned after his 1884 death, until **de Vries, Correns and Tschermak** independently rediscovered his 1866 paper in **spring 1900** (35 years later).
- **Schedule & hand-off continuity:** Follows `011` *Anesthesia* (10 Dec, medical lane) and `013` *Darwin & Wallace* (17 Dec). `013` Part 06 closes on Fleeming Jenkin's blending-paint objection and Mendel's unread pea-plant answer; `012` picks up that exact baton on Thu 24 Dec and can hand off either back to `013` (*Evolution: The Letter That Forced Darwin to Publish*, then live) or forward to `014` (*Mary Anning's Fossils*).
- **Working title pair (for 2-way Test & Compare):**
  - **Title A (Main — Subject + hook, §1 rule 11, 49 chars):** *Genetics: The Monk Who Counted 28,000 Pea Plants*
  - **Title B (Test & Compare — Search promise + exact number, §1 rules 2 & 4, 49 chars):** *How a Monk and 28,000 Pea Plants Solved Genetics*
  - **Title C (Alt — TED-Ed neighbour phrasing, 39 chars):** *How Mendel's Pea Plants Solved Heredity*
- **Thumbnail concept:** One hero object — a friar's hand holding tweezers and a fine paintbrush over an open green pea pod showing 3 golden smooth peas and 1 green wrinkled pea in warm abbey garden sunlight; Explorer small at lower left; 2–3 words of painted serif cover text that add to the title (*THE 3:1 SECRET* or *HIDDEN IN PEAS*, never repeating the title).
