### PART 02: 12 February 1892 & 1898 — The Porcelain Filter & Martinus Beijerinck's "Contagious Living Fluid" (`Virus`) (1:25–3:05)
- **Cold open (why in 1890 Louis Pasteur and Robert Koch's followers believed the Chamberland porcelain filter could sterilise any liquid on Earth — until mottled tobacco leaves broke the rule):** **Imperial Academy of Sciences, St. Petersburg, 12 February 1892 & Polytechnic School of Delft, Netherlands, 1898**.
- **12 February 1892 — Dmitri Ivanovsky's impossible filtrate:**
  - In **1884**, **Charles Chamberland** in **Louis Pasteur's (`001`)** Paris laboratory invented an unglazed porcelain water filter (`the Chamberland-Pasteur filter`) with pores so microscopic (`~100 to 200 nanometres`) that **not a single bacterium** could squeeze through.
  - Sent to Crimea and Ukraine to investigate a mysterious **"mosaic" disease** that mottled tobacco leaves and ruined harvests, 27-year-old Russian botanist **Dmitri Ivanovsky** (1864–1920) crushed infected leaves, poured the juice through a **Chamberland porcelain filter**, and rubbed the crystal-clear, bacteria-free liquid onto healthy leaves.
  - On **12 February 1892**, Ivanovsky reported to the St. Petersburg Academy of Sciences that the filtered liquid was **still 100% infectious** (`though Ivanovsky himself suspected a tiny bacterial spore or dissolved chemical toxin might have leaked through`).
- **1898 & 1915–1917 — Martinus Beijerinck coins the modern meaning of "Virus", and Twort & d'Hérelle find "Bacteria-Eaters" (`Bacteriophages`):**
  - Six years later in **Delft in 1898**, Dutch microbiologist **Martinus Beijerinck** (1851–1931) repeated the porcelain-filter test and did the crucial control that proved it **could not be a chemical poison**: he took a single filtered drop from an infected leaf, diluted it inside a second healthy plant, filtered that plant's juice, and passed it to a third and fourth plant — and its infectious power **never diluted away**! Furthermore, the agent diffused through solid **agar gel** (`where no cell could swim`), yet multiplied **only inside living, dividing plant cells**!
  - Beijerinck declared it a new form of nature — ***contagium vivum fluidum*** ("contagious living fluid") — and revived the Latin word **`virus`** (`meaning "slimy liquid" or "poison"`).
  - Soon **Friedrich Loeffler & Paul Frosch (1898)** found the first animal virus (`foot-and-mouth disease`), **Walter Reed (1901)** found the first human virus (`yellow fever`), and British bacteriologist **Frederick Twort (4 December 1915)** and French-Canadian microbiologist **Félix d'Hérelle (3 September 1917)** discovered invisible viruses that infect and blow up bacteria (**`bacteriophages`**, "bacteria-eaters")!
- *Markers:* `· WHO: Félix d'Hérelle (1873–1949) — French-Canadian microbiologist · WHEN: 1884 · WHEN: 12 February 1892 · WHEN: 1898 · WHEN: 4 December 1915 · WHEN: 3 September 1917 · FACT: Multiplied after passing through porcelain · DATA: inked Ivanovsky (1892) & Beijerinck (1898) serial-dilution vs chemical toxin chart on period paper showing a chemical toxin's potency dropping to zero across 10-fold serial dilutions while Beijerinck's porcelain-filtered Tobacco Mosaic Virus ("contagium vivum fluidum") amplifies exponentially back to 100% infectious titre inside each living host leaf · SCALE: if you drop a pinch of poison into a lake, it dilutes until it is harmless — yet one microscopic drop of Beijerinck's filtered liquid turned an entire field of healthy plants into factories making millions of new copies`

### PART 03: 28 June 1935 — Wendell Stanley Crystallises a Virus Like Table Salt & the Electron Microscope Sees It — Midpoint Twist (3:05–4:50)
- **Cold open ("Case closed — anything that reproduces inside living creatures must be a living cell! Wrong! - **28 June 1935 (*Science*) — Wendell Stanley's living crystals:**
  - Using the brand-new protein-crystallisation techniques of his Rockefeller colleague **John Howard Northrop** (`who had crystallised the stomach enzyme pepsin in 1930, 032`), 30-year-old chemist **Wendell Meredith Stanley** (1904–1971) ground up **over a ton of infected tobacco leaves**, treated the juice with ammonium sulfate, and on **28 June 1935** (*Science*, `sharing the 1946 Nobel Prize in Chemistry`) isolated **silky white needle crystals of pure `Tobacco Mosaic Virus` (`TMV`)**!
  - Dissolved at **`1 part in 1,000,000,000` (`one part per billion`)** and rubbed onto a healthy leaf, those "dead" mineral-like crystals erupted into full-blown mosaic disease — philosophical dynamite that made the front page of *The New York Times*!
- **December 1936 & 1938–1939 — Bawden & Pirie find the hidden 5% `RNA`, and Helmut Ruska takes the first photograph of a virus:**
  - Stanley initially thought his crystals were 100% pure protein. Eighteen months later, on **19 December 1936** (*Nature*), British researchers **Frederick Bawden** (1908–1972) and **Norman Pirie** (1907–1997) proved that every TMV crystal is actually **`95% protein` and `5% ribonucleic acid` (`RNA`)** — a genetic package wrapped in a protein coat (`whose helical structure Rosalind Franklin mapped by X-ray diffraction in 1955–1956, 016`)!
  - And in **1938–1939** in Berlin, physician **Helmut Ruska** (1908–1973, brother of electron-microscope inventor Ernst Ruska) and **Gustav Kausche** aimed a beam of electrons (`with a wavelength 100,000 times shorter than visible light!`) at TMV and bacteriophages — capturing humanity's **very first photographs of viruses** (`300-nanometre rods and tadpole-shaped phage syringes`)!

### PART 04: 20 September 1952 — Alfred Hershey, Martha Chase & the Radioactive Waring Blender Experiment (4:50–6:40)
- **Cold open (how two scientists at Cold Spring Harbor borrowed a Waring kitchen blender used for making milkshakes and proved how every virus on Earth hijacks living cells):** **Carnegie Institution Department of Genetics, Cold Spring Harbor, New York, 20 September 1952**.
- **The lunar-lander syringe (`T2 Bacteriophage`) & the radioactive tag:**
  - Under the electron microscope (`Thomas Anderson, 1950–1952`), **`T2` bacteriophages** look like tiny **lunar landers (`~200 nanometres tall`)** made of only two materials: a **protein outer shell (`50%`)** and a **DNA core (`50%`)**.
  - When `T2` lands on an ***E. coli* bacterium**, within **25 minutes** the bacterium bursts open (`lysis`), releasing **hundreds of brand-new viruses**.
  - At Cold Spring Harbor in **1951–1952** (*Journal of General Physiology*, **20 September 1952**, `1969 Nobel Prize`), **Alfred Hershey** (1908–1997) and 24-year-old research assistant **Martha Chase** (1927–2003) designed a brilliant two-batch radioactive trap (`building on nuclear isotopes from 023`):
    1. **Batch 1 (Glowing Protein):** Because **protein contains sulfur (`in methionine and cysteine`) but DNA contains zero sulfur**, they grew `T2` viruses in **radioactive sulfur (`³⁵S`)** so **only the protein coat glowed**.
    2. **Batch 2 (Glowing DNA):** Because **DNA is rich in phosphorus (`in its sugar-phosphate backbone, 016`) while protein contains almost none**, they grew a second batch in **radioactive phosphorus (`³²P`)** so **only the DNA core glowed**.
- **Switching on the Waring kitchen blender:**
  - Hershey and Chase let both batches of viruses attach to *E. coli* bacteria for a few minutes — then poured the cultures into a **high-speed Waring kitchen blender** (`spinning at 10,000 rpm`) to shear the empty virus coats off the outside of the bacterial cells without popping the bacteria!
  - When they spun the mixture in a centrifuge:
    - **`80%` of the radioactive sulfur (`³⁵S` protein coats) stayed outside in the liquid soup** — proving the protein coat is just a **disposable hypodermic syringe** left at the door!
    - The **radioactive phosphorus (`³²P` DNA) went straight inside the bacteria** (`and into the next generation of baby viruses`) — proving beyond doubt that **pure genetic code (`DNA`) is the master blueprint that hijacks the cell**!

Physiol. 36: 39–56) plotting blender agitation time (0 to 8 minutes) vs extracellular 35S protein coat sheared into the supernatant (~80%) while ~70–85% of 32P viral DNA remains inside the infected E. coli pellet to direct progeny phage assembly · SCALE: the Hershey-Chase blender experiment proved that a virus protein coat is nothing more than a disposable delivery van — it parks on the outside of your cell wall, injects its DNA or RNA instructions inside, and gets left behind`
