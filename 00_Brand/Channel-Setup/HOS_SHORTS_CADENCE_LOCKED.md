# History of Science — Shorts cadence (locked recommendation)

**Date:** 26 Aug 2026 · Ben asked: daily Shorts vs other?  
**Answer:** **Not daily.** Use a **pillar cluster** (HeyHistorically habit + small-channel reality).

## Recommendation

| Layer | Cadence |
|---|---|
| **Long** | **1× / week** · Thursday **19:00 UK** · **8–9 min** |
| **Shorts** | **4–6 per long** over the **7–10 days after** the long goes public |
| **Spacing** | Day 1 evening Short #1 (~2h after long) · then **one Short every 1–2 days** · never dump the cluster |
| **Related** | Every public Short → Studio **Related ▶** that week’s film title |
| **Do not** | Daily forever · Shorts before the long is public · orphan Shorts with no parent |

### Why not daily (for this channel)

1. **New channel** — daily Shorts without a strong long behind them pads catalogue and burns Veo/VO capacity.
2. **HeyHistorically evidence** — ~2 longs/month + ~weekly Shorts *around* pillars, not a Shorts-only treadmill.
3. **Animistry-class longs** need craft; Shorts exist to **sell the 8–9 min film**, not replace it.
4. Growth System already: Shorts are **cluster entry points** (4–8), punch-first **22–27s**.

### When to reconsider daily

Only after: consistent weekly long shipping · Shorts → long clicks visible · hold on the 8–9 min film past ~5 min · spare production capacity. Until then: **cluster > calendar spam**.
