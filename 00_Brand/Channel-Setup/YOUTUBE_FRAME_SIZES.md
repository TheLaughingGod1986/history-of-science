# YouTube frame sizes

Set 25 Sep 2026. Cursor rule: `.cursor/rules/hos-studio.mdc`.

## Long-form

- 1920 × 1080, 16:9, 30 fps constant (CFR), H.264 MP4
- 2560 × 1440 or 3840 × 2160 when a higher-quality master is made

## Shorts

- 1080 × 1920, 9:16
- YouTube allows up to 3 minutes. HOS Shorts stay 22–27 seconds, and the ship gate fails anything 40 s or longer.

## Graphics

- Long thumbnail: 1280 × 720, 16:9, under 2 MB
- Short cover: 1080 × 1920, hook centre-safe for the 16:9 crop (`THUMBNAIL_AND_TITLE_RULES.md` §4)
- Channel banner: 2560 × 1440, text in the centre safe area
- Profile picture: 800 × 800
