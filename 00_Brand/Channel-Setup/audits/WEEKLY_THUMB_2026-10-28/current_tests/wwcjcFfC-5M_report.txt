The Tied Arm That Proved Your Blood Circulates
A/B testing
Description
Your heart pumps more blood in half an hour than your whole body holds. So where does it all go?
For 1,400 years doctors taught that blood was made from food and used up, until William Harvey proved it circulates, with a sum and a band tied round an arm.

This film is how he did it: Galen's liver, Fabricius's little doors in the veins, Harvey's sum at the Royal College of Physicians, the tied arm in De Motu Cordis (1628), and the capillaries Malpighi found in 1661.
