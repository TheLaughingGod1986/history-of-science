## Primary strategy

**Every long-form video is the pillar.**

Every Short exists to **support, promote, or expand** that pillar.

The long-form video is **always** the priority for:

- Watch time
- Subscriber conversion
- Suggested / browse authority
- Monetisation readiness

Shorts are a discovery engine. They are never the product.

```
Pillar documentary (Day 1)
        ↓
Premium mini-doc Shorts (Day 1 evening → Day 7)
        ↓
Shorts feed discovery
        ↓
Related / soft CTA → pillar long-form
        ↓
Watch time · subs · returning viewers
        ↓
Suggested for next Thursday pillar
```

## Publishing order (every release)

### Day 1 — Pillar first

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Publish the **long-form** first |
| 2 | Window: **18:00–20:00 UK** (ops lock: **19:00**) |
| 3 | Title, description, chapters, thumbnail, end screens, cards — **final before go-live** |

### Day 1 — First Short (1–3 hours later)

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 4 | Publish Short #1 from the **strongest hook** or most intriguing moment |
| 5 | Ops lock: **21:00 UK** (≈ 2 hours after the long) |
| 6 | Related video / pinned comment → this week’s long-form |

### Days 2–7 — One Short per day

Publish **one Short per day**.

Each Short explores a **different idea** from the long-form.

| Day | Relative | Time UK | Role |
|-----|----------|---------|------|
| 1 | Thu | 19:00 | **Long-form pillar** |
| 1 | Thu | 21:00 | Short #1 — strongest hook |
| 2 | Fri | 12:30 | Short #2 — named mystery / fact |
| 3 | Sat | 12:30 | Short #3 — scale / visceral beat |
| 4 | Sun | 12:30 | Short #4 — theory / twist |
| 5 | Mon | 12:30 | Short #5 — secondary wonder |
| 6 | Tue | 12:30 | Short #6 — Orbit reaction / deeper cut |
| 7 | Wed | 12:30 | Short #7 *(optional)* — closer / invitation |

**Cluster size:** **5–7 Shorts** per long (default ops: **6**; V001 ships with **5** already scripted).

**Never** publish a teaser Short *before* the pillar is public.  
**Never** dump the whole cluster on Day 1.

## Shorts strategy

### Do not cut random clips

Each Short must work as a **complete standalone mini-documentary**.

### Every Short must

1. Hook within the **first 2 seconds** (on-screen + VO)
2. Focus on **one** fascinating fact, mystery, or question
3. Deliver value **independently** of the long
4. End with **curiosity**
5. Naturally encourage the full documentary — without sounding like an ad

### Arc (30–60s)

```
0–2s   Hook (question / paradox / scale)
2–40s  Single idea · cinematic · Orbit guide beats
40–60s Curiosity ending + soft funnel line
```

### Soft endings (preferred)

Use calm, documentary language:

- “If you want the full story, it’s now available on Orbit With Ben.”
- “This is only one part of a much bigger mystery explored in our latest documentary.”
- “The full story unfolds in our latest film — Will We Ever Meet Aliens?”

Avoid: “WATCH NOW”, “LINK IN BIO” energy, hard-sell captions, fake urgency.

### Quality bar

Every Short should feel like a **premium mini-documentary**:

- Burned-in captions
- Vertical 1080×1920
- Orbit present as guide, not decoration
- Same science voice as the long
- No conspiracy framing

## Production system (sustainable)

### Pre-release (by Wednesday night)

- [ ] Long picture-locked, VO final, thumb final
- [ ] Chapters + description + tags drafted
- [ ] 5–7 Short scripts locked from the long’s idea map
- [ ] Short #1–#N VO generated
- [ ] Long scheduled private → public Thu 19:00
- [ ] Shorts uploaded private / scheduled
- [ ] Flywheel copy drafted (social + Community)

### Release week

Follow `RELEASE_WEEK_CHECKLIST.md`.

### Next-week overlap

While Days 5–7 Shorts of week N publish, week N+1 long is already in VO / edit. Never start next week’s long on Thursday morning.

## Metadata principles

Always prioritise:

- Viewer retention
- Curiosity
- Storytelling
- Educational value
- Consistency
- High-quality thumbnails
- Strong titles
- Search-friendly metadata
- Evergreen topics

**Titles:** Question + Mystery + Emotion  
**Thumbs:** one idea · ≤ ~4 words on-image · Orbit only if he raises curiosity  
**Descriptions:** hook paragraph → summary → chapters → soft CTA → sources

## Analytics (Monday 30 min)

Score last pillar + its Shorts cluster:

| Metric | Healthy |
|--------|---------|
| Long CTR | 5–10% |
| Long AVD | 40%+ |
| Short → long traffic | Rising after Day 1 Short |
| Subs / long view | Climbing week over week |
| Watch time mix | Dominated by **longs**, not Shorts alone |

**Kill rules**

- Thumb CTR &lt; 3% after ~1k impressions → replace
- Long AVD &lt; 30% → reopen edit (do not lengthen)
- Short swipe-away high vs peers → retire that hook style

---

## Guardrails

- Long-form first. Always.
- Shorts are mini-docs, not ads or random cuts.
- Soft curiosity CTAs only.
- Science over speculation; wonder over certainty.
- Family-friendly; no conspiracy bait.
- One idea per Short; one story spine per long.
