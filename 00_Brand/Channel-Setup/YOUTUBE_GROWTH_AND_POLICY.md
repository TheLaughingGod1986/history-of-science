# YouTube growth and policy

Set 25 Sep 2026, from Orbit With Ben's version of the same date. Cursor rule: `.cursor/rules/hos-studio.mdc`.

The algorithm follows the audience.

## Get more views

- Picture already moving: a long in the first 3 seconds, a Short in the first second. No intro card.
- The thumbnail grabs attention; the title creates curiosity. They are not the same words, and both match the film.
- A live long under about 4% CTR (past about 500 impressions) needs a new thumb or title. Stay in the 4–7% band without breaking the promise.
- Cut pauses, frozen stills and fluff. Aim for an average percentage viewed above about 50%.

## Keep them watching

- First 30 seconds: start on the promised picture. Sentence one is the promise. No logo, no theme, no "welcome to History of Science".
- About every 4–6 seconds, change the picture, put the named thing on screen, or bring in a label.
- Tell it as cause and effect. A list of facts loses people.
- No goodbye. Don't say "in conclusion", "to wrap up" or "thanks for watching". Finish the last point, hand off to the next film, and let the end screen do the rest.

## Stay in good standing

- No music, film clips or footage we don't have the rights to.
- Titles, tags and thumbnails match the film.
- Altered or synthetic content disclosure: yes, on every upload.
- History as it happened: no invented quotes, no made-up dates. Sources under every long.
- No gore, no medical advice. Old medicine is shown as history, not as something to try.

## Consistency

One long a week and three Shorts. A finished film beats a rushed one.
