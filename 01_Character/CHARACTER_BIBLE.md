# History of Science — Explorer (character bible)

**Locked:** 25 Aug 2026  
**Canonical sheet:** `01_Character/01_Master-References/hos-explorer-character-sheet-v01.jpg`  
**Gen reference:** `01_Character/05_Generation-References/hos-explorer-reference-v01.jpg`

Working name until Ben locks a proper name: **the Explorer**.

## Role in the film (hard)

- He is a **side character**, not the star.
- The **story / discovery** is the main picture. Scenery and ideas lead.
- Appear **every few scenes only** — roughly one beat every 3–5 scenes, not continuous Orbit-style presence.
- When he appears: walk through the world, show interest, lightly interact with 1–2 props, then exit. Do not park him centre-frame for whole VO blocks.
- Mute test: if you mute VO, you still follow the **discovery story**; he is colour, not narration.

## Look (lock)

3D cartoon / feature-animation polish (not flat 2D, not photoreal).

- Young boy · messy wavy brown hair · large expressive eyes · round thin gold wire-rim glasses
- Teal-blue long overcoat · gold atom pin on lapel · tan waistcoat · white shirt · dark brown floppy bow tie
- Brown trousers rolled at cuffs · cream socks · sturdy brown lace-up boots
- Brown leather satchel · brass compass on strap · rolled parchment map in coat pocket
- Signature prop: thick blue book with gold cover line **IDEAS · OBSERVE · QUESTION · DISCOVER**

### Expression kit (from sheet)

| Beat | Pose |
|---|---|
| Hero / establish | Book in hand, confident stance |
| Observe | Brass telescope, excited smile |
| Think / deep explain | Open book + quill, finger on chin |
| Eureka / discovery | Point up · glowing gold atom above finger |

## On-screen behaviour

Good:
- Library: walks aisle → picks dusty book → blows dust → opens / reads along with VO
- Lab / workshop: leans in, tips a tool, reacts surprised / curious / thinking
- Outdoor discovery: walks path, pauses at artifact, telescope beat
- Deep science VO: cut to think pose or soft “wow” eyes — then back to the **subject**

Bad:
- Every scene
- Talking-head Orbit substitute dominating the frame
- Dual Explorers / clone / reflection
- Redesign clothes, hair, age, or glasses mid-episode
- Readable UI text other than the locked book title

## Style lock for the channel

- Picture default: **3D cartoon animated** worlds (rich colour, soft cinematic light, stylised materials)
- Upbeat, curious, bright — not dread essay / photoreal space doc
- Orbit orange robot is **not** this channel’s mascot (`01_Orbit-Character/` = archive seed only)
