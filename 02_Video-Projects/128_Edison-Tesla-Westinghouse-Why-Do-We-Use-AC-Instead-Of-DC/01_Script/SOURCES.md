## Primary & Authoritative Sources (for Long Description `Sources:` Line)

1. **U.S. Patent No. 381,968 (1 May 1888)** — Nikola Tesla, *"Electro-Magnetic Motor"* (polyphase alternating-current induction motor and rotating magnetic field).  
   URL: `https://patents.google.com/patent/US381968A/en`
2. **IEEE Milestone (1886) & Engineering and Technology History Wiki (ETHW)** — William Stanley Jr. and George Westinghouse, Great Barrington, Massachusetts AC transformer demonstration (20 March 1886, 500 V → 3,000 V → 100 V over 4,000 feet).  
   URL: `https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Stanley_Jr.` · `https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/War_of_the_currents`
3. **Smithsonian Institution / Pearl Street Station History (1882)** — Thomas Edison's Pearl Street Station launch (4 September 1882, 110 V DC, 400 lamps, 82 customers, <1 mile reach).  
   URL: `https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pearl_Street_Station`
4. **Ganz Works / ZBD Closed-Core Parallel Transformer (Budapest, 1885)** — Károly Zipernowsky, Ottó Bláthy, and Miksa Déri (preceded by Lucien Gaulard and John Dixon Gibbs, 1882–1884).  
   URL: `https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alternating_current`
5. **Galileo Ferraris (March–April 1888, Turin)** — Independent discovery and publication of the rotating magnetic field (*Rotazioni elettrodinamiche prodotte per mezzo di correnti alternate*, Royal Academy of Science, Turin, 1888).  
   URL: `https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galileo_Ferraris`
6. **World's Columbian Exposition (Chicago, May 1893)** — Westinghouse $399,000 vs General Electric $554,000 lighting bid, planned 93,000 incandescent lamps, and Sawyer-Man "stopper lamp".  
   URL: `https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World%27s_Columbian_Exposition`
7. **National Park Service / National Historic Landmark & IEEE Milestone (1895–1896)** — Edward Dean Adams Power Plant, Niagara Falls (5,000 hp Westinghouse-Tesla polyphase AC generators, local start 25 August 1895; 11,000 V transmission ~22 miles to Buffalo, 15–16 November 1896).  
   URL: `https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adams_Power_Plant_Transformer_House`
8. **Joule Heating & High-Voltage Direct Current (HVDC)** — $P = V I$ and resistive loss $P_{\text{loss}} = I^2 R$; modern solid-state HVDC subsea and long-distance transmission.  
   URL: `https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High-voltage_direct_current`
