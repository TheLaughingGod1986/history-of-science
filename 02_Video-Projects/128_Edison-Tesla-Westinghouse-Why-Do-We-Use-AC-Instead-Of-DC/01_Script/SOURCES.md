1. **Thomas A. Edison Papers, Rutgers University (1882 & 1892 Chronologies)** — Holborn Viaduct London central station (`12 January 1882`), Pearl Street Station launch (`4 September 1882`), and General Electric Company organization (`15 April 1892`).
   URLs: `https://edison.rutgers.edu/edit-chronology/chronology-details/1881---1890/1882` · `https://edison.rutgers.edu/edit-chronology/chronology-details/1891---1900/1892`
2. **IEEE Milestone — Pearl Street Station, 1882 (Engineering and Technology History Wiki / ETHW)** — `257 Pearl Street` (`255–257`), afternoon of `4 September 1882`, `110 V DC`, six `100 kW` dynamos, `~400` lamps, `~85` customers, `0.25 sq mi` First District, `~80,000 ft` twin half-moon underground copper conductors; plus Thomas Edison National Historical Park (NPS) biography.
   URLs: `https://ethw.org/Milestones:Pearl_Street_Station,_1882` · `https://www.nps.gov/edis/learn/historyculture/edison-biography.htm`
3. **IEEE Milestone — Alternating Current Electrification, 1886 (ETHW) & ETHW Biographies (William Stanley, George Westinghouse, Nikola Tesla, AC vs. DC)** — Great Barrington, MA (`20 March 1886`, old rubber mill): `500 V → 3,000 V → 500 V` step-up/step-down demonstration (30 series lamps) and `500 V` Main Street overhead distribution via `6` basement transformers to `100 V` for `20` businesses; 1886 Frank Sprague report; Westinghouse air brake; Tesla biography.
   URLs: `https://ethw.org/Milestones:Alternating_Current_Electrification,_1886` · `https://ethw.org/William_Stanley` · `https://ethw.org/Westinghouse_Electric_Corporation` · `https://ethw.org/Nikola_Tesla` · `https://ethw.org/AC_vs._DC`
4. **U.S. Patent No. 381,968 (1 May 1888, via Google Patents)** — Nikola Tesla, *"Electro-Magnetic Motor"* (filed `12 October 1887`, granted `1 May 1888`, polyphase AC induction motor).
   URL: `https://patents.google.com/patent/US381968A/en`
5. **U.S. Department of Energy (DOE, 2014) — *The War of the Currents: AC vs. DC Power*** — Edison's DC royalty defense, 1893 Chicago World's Fair bids (`$554,000` GE vs `$399,000` Westinghouse), Niagara Falls power to Buffalo (`Nov. 16, 1896`), GE adopting AC, and modern DC/HVDC resurgence.
   URL: `https://www.energy.gov/articles/war-currents-ac-vs-dc-power`
6. **National Park Service (NPS) National Register of Historic Places Nomination Form — *Adams Power Plant Transformer House* (NRIS `75001212`) & IEEE Milestone — *Adams Hydroelectric Generating Plant, 1895* (ETHW)** — 1891 European polyphase trials, 1893 Chicago showcase, `27 October 1893` Westinghouse contract for `5,000-hp` polyphase AC generators, **`26 August 1895`** local operation start, and **`15 November 1896`** long-distance AC transmission **`22 miles`** to Buffalo, NY *(variance note: ETHW `Westinghouse_Electric_Corporation` states `20 miles` — flagged for independent `facts` review)*.
   URLs: `https://npgallery.nps.gov/AssetDetail/NRIS/75001212` · `https://npgallery.nps.gov/GetAsset/bccab15f-4fe0-4909-82a6-ca499a8883c2` · `https://ethw.org/Milestones:Adams_Hydroelectric_Generating_Plant,_1895`
7. **U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA)** — North American AC grid frequency (`60 cycles per second (hertz)`) and substation step-up / step-down voltage delivery.
   URLs: `https://www.eia.gov/electricity/930-content/about.html` · `https://www.eia.gov/energyexplained/electricity/delivery-to-consumers.php`
8. **U.S. Department of Energy OSTI — *Manhattan Project: Safety and the Trinity Test, July 1945*** — Final handoff line: Los Alamos scientists checking whether the New Mexico atomic test could ignite the atmosphere.
