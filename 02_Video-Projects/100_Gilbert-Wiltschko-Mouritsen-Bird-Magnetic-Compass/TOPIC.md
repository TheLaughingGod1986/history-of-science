### 2.1 Core Packaging

- **Title B (Test & Compare — Statement):** `The Quantum Compass Hidden Inside a Robin's Eye` *(47 chars)*
- **Title C (Experiment Question):** `How a Caged Robin Proved Birds See Magnetism` *(44 chars)*
- **One-Line Hook:** Every autumn, a thumb-sized European robin flies `4,000` kilometres in the dark to a forest it has never seen—guided by a quantum-entangled magnetic compass inside its own eye.
- **Thumbnail Concept:** Close 3D house-style portrait of a wide-eyed European robin in night flight; reflected inside its dark pupil is a glowing blue-arc magnetic grid of Earth, with a 3-word Didot cover label `HE SEES NORTH` (or `QUANTUM / EYE`). Passes `168×94` mobile legibility (`LEARNINGS.md` Rule 4): one dominant subject, high-contrast warm orange breast vs deep indigo night sky, zero title duplication.

### 2.2 Person, Date & Twist (Historical Discovery Chain + Teach-on-Screen Plan)
1. **William Gilbert (`1600`, London — *De Magnete*):** Queen Elizabeth I's physician carves a spherical lodestone (`terrella`, "little Earth") and moves a tiny iron needle across its surface, proving that Earth itself is a giant magnet whose field lines dip downward toward the poles (`WHO: William Gilbert (1544–1603) — Royal physician & physicist`, `WHEN: London, 1600`).
2. **Alexander von Middendorff (`1855`) & Stephen Emlen (`1966`, Cornell):** Middendorff proposes birds might sense Earth's magnetism, which physicists dismiss for over a century because Earth's field (`~50 microteslas`, **`100×` weaker than a fridge magnet**) carries far too little energy to trigger a nerve cell (`DATA: Earth's field 50 µT vs fridge magnet 5,000 µT · SCALE: 100 times weaker than a fridge magnet`). In `1966`, Stephen Emlen invents the **Emlen funnel**—a blotter-paper cone with an ink pad at the bottom that records a restless migrating bird's hop marks (`WHEN: Ithaca, 1966`).
