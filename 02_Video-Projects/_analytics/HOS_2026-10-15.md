## 2. What's working and what isn't (with evidence)

Viewers arriving from an education neighbour on the exact same scientist/discovery watch nearly twice as long as Browse viewers (`97 views, 14 min` = 9 s average).
2. **Periodic Table (`AL_-qlWko_g`) retention and CTR are inching up as Suggested & Search share grows:**
   - Compared with the 12 Oct audit, `AL_-qlWko_g` improved at every checkpoint: **0:15 rose `72% → 74%`, 0:30 rose `55% → 56%`, 1:00 rose `45% → 48%`, 2:00 reached `26%`, and End rose `12% → 13%`**, while lifetime CTR improved from **`3.0%` (11 Oct) → `3.4%` (15 Oct, 944 impressions)**.
3. **Search-titled Shorts win both `stayedToWatch` and lifetime retention:**
   - Shorts with a clear discovery claim or `How / Why` question (`He predicted a metal before it was found` **65.2% stayed**, `Why tellurium sat before iodine` **57.7% stayed**, `How X-rays Were Discovered by Accident` **45.0% stayed / 67.2% AVD / +1 sub**) beat negative/statement Shorts (`Germs don't cast a shadow` **18.8% stayed**, `Germs hitch a ride on you` **19.2% stayed**, `Invisible life is still everywhere` **25.0% stayed**) by **20 to 47 percentage points on `stayedToWatch`**.

1. **Shorts feed bursts are leaking 75–81% of viewers at frame 0 (`stayedToWatch: 18.8%–28.6%`):**
   - Whenever YouTube pushes a HOS Short into the Shorts feed (`qOZgess30h0` 166 views, `iqToagXnjX0` 110 views, `H1y0DXFVmw8` 78 views), **71% to 81% of feed viewers swipe away before 2 seconds** (`stayedToWatch`: `25.0%`, `28.6%`, `18.8%`). On a channel under 2,000 impressions per video, **2-variant tests (A vs B, 50/50 split)** double the impressions per variant and reach a verdict twice as fast.
