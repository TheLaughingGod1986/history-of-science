## Generation strategy

1. **Hero rooms first (reusable):** observatory · watches-science desk · deep-space void  
2. **Spectacle seconds:** accretion approach · horizon crossing · galactic jets · Kerr near-miss  
3. **Reaction inserts:** myth-swipe · stretch gag · clock split · closing wave  
4. Prefer **8–10s** continuous character motion clips per beat; stitch under VO rather than freezing stills.  
5. Science content on holograms/screens where possible so Orbit remains the emotional subject.
