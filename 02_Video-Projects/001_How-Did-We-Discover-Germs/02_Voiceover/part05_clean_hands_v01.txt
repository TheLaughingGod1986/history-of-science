Joseph Lister took the new idea into the bloodiest room in medicine.
If microbes seed wounds, then kill them on contact. Spray. Scrub. Sterilise what you can. Suddenly successful operations stop ending in fever and ruin.
Prevention becomes a craft. Not a prayer. Not a perfume. A protocol.
What would you feel as a patient, watching a surgeon wash as if the enemy were real?
And you live inside that victory every time soap meets your hands.
The invisible enemy became a map. Once mapped, it could be fought.
If an entire civilisation can miss a world living in every breath, what else is still invisible to us?
