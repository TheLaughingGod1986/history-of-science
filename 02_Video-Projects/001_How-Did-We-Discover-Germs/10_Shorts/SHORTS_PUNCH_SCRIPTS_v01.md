# Shorts punch scripts — Germs cluster (draft VO · 22–27s each)

Parent long: **How Did We Discover Germs?**  
Build after long picture locks. British Ben Orbit Narrator. Punch-first.

---

## S01 — Doctors Blamed Bad Air — Not Germs

What if the cleanest hospital still killed you?
Doctors once blamed “bad air” — the smell — not living germs.
Hands moved from body to body. Instruments shone. Fever still rose.
The enemy had no shadow… until science proved it was alive.
Full film: How Did We Discover Germs?

---

## S02 — The First Time Humans Saw Microbes

A drop of water is not empty.
Under the lens it becomes a city — rods, spheres, life with no name on the chart.
Seeing the tiny world was step one.
Believing it caused disease took far longer.
How did we discover germs?

## S03 — Why Handwashing Was Once “Ridiculous”

What if washing your hands was mocked as nonsense?
Semmelweis cut deaths by scrubbing — and colleagues rejected him.
The data embarrassed the profession.
Proof had to get louder than pride.
The full story is in How Did We Discover Germs?

---

## S04 — Pasteur’s Flask That Broke the Old Story

A flask shaped like a question mark.
Boil the broth. Keep the neck intact — it stays clear.
Break the trap — life blooms into cloudiness.
Passengers from outside. Not magic from nothing.
That demo helped flip medicine forever.

---

## S05 — The Spray That Cleaned the Operating Theatre

If germs seed wounds, kill them on contact.
Lister’s spray turned “successful” surgery into survival.
Prevention became a craft — not a perfume.
You live inside that victory every time soap meets your hands.
Watch How Did We Discover Germs?
