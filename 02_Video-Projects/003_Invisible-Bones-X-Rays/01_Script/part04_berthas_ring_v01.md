Proof needs a witness the world cannot argue with.

Bertha Röntgen places her hand in the beam. The plate records bones — and her wedding ring. When people see that image, the haunted glow becomes a fact.

Letters fly. Labs copy the tube. Doctors lean in. A bullet lodged where eyes cannot go. A break that used to be a guess. The body becomes a map you can read while the patient is still whole.

Why is that groundbreaking? Surgery is no longer the only way to look inside. Diagnosis gains a new eye.
