Then the leap that still feels like science fiction.

A hand in the path. Soft flesh fades in the glow. The bones hold. A denser shape — a ring — sits darker still.

You are looking at a living skeleton. No knife. No pain. Just a new kind of light.

X-rays pass soft tissue more than bone. That is why the skeleton appears without cutting anyone open.

What happens to medicine the day you can see a break before you cut?

Fear arrives with wonder. Invisible light that writes bones on cardboard can also burn if you stay too long. Power and danger travel together — a theme medicine will learn the hard way.
