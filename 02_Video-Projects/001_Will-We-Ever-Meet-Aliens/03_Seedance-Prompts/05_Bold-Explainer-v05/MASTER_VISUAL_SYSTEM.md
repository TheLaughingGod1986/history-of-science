# Bold Explainer v05 — Master Visual System

## Format

- 24 coordinated styleboards.
- Four unique 16:9 scene compositions per styleboard.
- 96 unique visual sources in total.
- Approximate visual-source duration: 10–15 seconds each.
- Each source receives internal animation, reframing, or layered parallax so it
  does not behave as a frozen still.
- Exact source reuse is prohibited.
- A returning idea must use a new composition and a new asset ID.

## Visual identity

Use the approved bold explainer test as the canonical style:

- hand-painted editorial science illustration
- deep navy and petrol teal base
- cyan scientific light
- warm cream and restrained Orbit-orange accents
- subtle paper grain
- flowing data lines and elegant diagrammatic structures
- adult, premium, mysterious, and scientifically responsible

Do not embed Orbit into generated scene art. Orbit is a separate approved
animated overlay.

Avoid:

- generated text
- humanoid aliens
- generic flying saucers
- fantasy spacecraft used as filler
- repeated galaxies or planets
- photorealistic stock-footage discontinuity
- visual activity that competes with the narration

## Opening timing

1. Scenes 001–004 form the uninterrupted 14–18 second hook.
2. Hold the channel brand card for approximately two seconds.
3. Resume immediately on “This is Orbit.”
4. Do not place another logo animation inside the body of the film.

## Animation treatment

Every illustrated source must receive at least three of the following:

- slow camera push or drift
- foreground/background parallax
- orbit-line motion
- controlled particle movement
- spectrum or waveform growth
- light travelling along a path
- atmospheric or cloud movement
- telescope or instrument movement
- small sequential reveal
- Orbit eye, antenna, hover, head, or arm movement

Animation must clarify the idea. It must not be constant decoration.

## Orbit choreography

- Default stage: lower right, inside safe margins.
- Secondary stage: lower left only when the focal subject requires it.
- Never jump corners between consecutive shots.
- Move between stages with an intentional fly-across transition.
- Typical presence: 35–45% of runtime.
- Typical continuous appearance: 8–20 seconds.
- Do not flash Orbit on for only one or two seconds.
- Orbit should look towards the scientific subject, then occasionally towards
  the audience.

## No-repeat validation

Before the review export:

1. Hash every source image and source video.
2. Fail the build if the same hash appears in more than one timeline segment.
3. Record each source path, start time, end time, and asset ID.
4. Visually review near-duplicates that hashes cannot catch.
5. Reject flipped, recoloured, or speed-adjusted copies used to simulate variety.

The only reusable components permitted are:

- the approved Orbit rig
- the brand card
- typography rules
- transition grammar
- music motifs

Their individual timeline performances must still be intentionally varied.
