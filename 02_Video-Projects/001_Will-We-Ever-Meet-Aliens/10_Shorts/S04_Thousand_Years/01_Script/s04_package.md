## 3. Full script (~105 words · ~42s)

**[0–3s HOOK]**  
Even a hello from a thousand light-years away… takes a thousand years to arrive.

**[3–35s]**  
That’s not poetry. That’s logistics.  
Light is fast — but the galaxy is rude about distance.  
If someone sent a radio message today from a “nearby” star system on the cosmic scale, your great-great-great-grandchildren might hear it.  
A ship crawling at a fraction of light speed could outlast empires, languages… maybe even species.  
So “meeting” might never mean a handshake.  
It might mean a molecule in an atmosphere. A line on a graph. Evidence that someone existed.

**[35–45s END]**  
Not a visitor. A fingerprint.  
I’m Orbit — the full distance problem is in our longer film.
