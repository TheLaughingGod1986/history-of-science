## 3. Full script (~110 words · ~44s)

**[0–3s HOOK]**  
Maybe the universe is silent because nobody is there.

**[3–36s]**  
Or maybe it’s silent because everyone is waiting for someone to ask the first question.  
We don’t know if we’ll ever meet aliens face to… faceplate.  
A handshake across the stars is hard.  
But a biosignature? A strange atmosphere? A signal that finally repeats?  
This century is one of the best windows we’ve ever had to find out.  
Looking for life out there is also a way of understanding life here — how rare it might be… how precious a noisy little planet becomes when the sky stays quiet.

**[36–48s END]**  
The silence isn’t empty. It’s an invitation to keep listening.  
I’m Orbit. Full film’s on the channel — stay curious.
