Are we alone in the universe? The Fermi Paradox explained — if the Milky Way holds hundreds of billions of stars and countless planets, why does the sky stay so quiet?

In this Orbit's Cosmic Journey film, Orbit explores the Fermi Paradox, the Great Filter, the zoo hypothesis, the limits of interstellar communication, and how humanity might detect alien life. The first convincing sign may not be a spacecraft or a dramatic message. It may be a faint chemical fingerprint, an unusual pulse, or a clue already hidden inside old data.
