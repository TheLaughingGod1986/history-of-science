## 4. `J3264` Scientific & Historical Guardrails Locked Into Packaging & Script

1. **Smeaton coefficient math:** John Smeaton's published `0.005` pressure coefficient vs the Wrights' `0.0033` (modern NASA derivation `0.00327`) means `0.005` is **roughly 50% too high** (`(0.005 − 0.0033) / 0.0033 ≈ 51.5%`, or `0.0033` is about one-third lower than `0.005`). Never use `40% TOO HIGH`.
2. **Accurate aerodynamic lift (no equal-transit-time myth):** Explicitly reject or avoid the debunked "equal transit time" fallacy (`NASA GRC Bernoulli & Newton`). Explain lift accurately through the curved/tilted wing turning airflow downward (Newton reaction) and creating lower pressure above and higher pressure below (Bernoulli pressure difference), verified empirically on the Wrights' wind-tunnel balances.
3. **Geography precision:** Name **Kill Devil Hills, near Kitty Hawk, North Carolina** for the gliding and 17 December 1903 flights (Kitty Hawk was the nearby fishing village and post/telegraph station ~4 miles north of the dunes).
