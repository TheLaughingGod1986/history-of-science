Your heart pumps more blood in half an hour than your whole body holds. For 1,400 years doctors taught that blood was made fresh from food and used up, until William Harvey proved it circulates.

This film is how he did it: Galen's liver, Fabricius's little doors in the veins, Harvey's sum at the Royal College of Physicians, a band tied round an arm, and the capillaries Malpighi found in 1661.
