## Rules for every plate

- **House look (`STUDIO_PLAYBOOK.md` §5):** Animistry-class premium 3D cartoon, moving from frame 0, never stills or Ken Burns. Silent picture; VO, music bed, and `sfx_kit.py` stems (`rumble_low`, `whoosh_soft`, `tick_ink`) are mixed in the edit.
- **No readable text in a Veo plate.** All `LABEL`, `WHO`, `WHEN`, `DATA`, `SCALE`, and `FACT` text is composited in the edit in white Didot italic or a soft cream-on-brown teach card (`AGENTS.md` → *Teach on screen*). Notebooks, book spines, letters, and reports in Veo prompts carry suggested ink lines only, never legible lettering.
- **One label or card at a time, 4 words or fewer per label.** Never two full-frame code/teach graphics back to back: a moving 3D plate always sits between them.
- **People (`AGENTS.md` Reference lessons §1):** Every named person gets a `WHO` card at first mention (name on line 1, sourced years and period-accurate role of ≤4 words on line 2, lower third, 3–4 s) plus a credited archive portrait cut-out with gentle parallax where available.
  - **Darwin's age by period:** aged **26** on the Galápagos in Sept 1835 (clean-shaven with sideburns, wide-brimmed hat); aged **28–29** in London in 1837–1838; aged **35–45** at Down House during the 1844 essay and 1846–1854 barnacle years; aged **49–50** with full beard in 1858–1859.
  - **Wallace's age (`J0307` fix #5):** born 8 Jan 1823 → aged **29** when the *Helen* sank in Aug 1852, and aged **35** (mid-thirties, bearded, round wire-rimmed spectacles) in the Malay Archipelago in Feb–June 1858. Never depict him as a teenager or boy.
- **Chronology locks (`J0307`):**
  1. Part 03 (`3:54–4:14`): July 1844 230-page essay and 5 July 1844 letter to Emma come **before** October 1844 *Vestiges* (`"A few months later..."`).
  2. Part 04 (`5:50–6:00`): 25 June 1858 fairness letter to Lyell comes **before** Charles Waring Darwin's death three days later (28 June 1858). No child illness shown on screen — show a darkened Down House window and grieving Darwin at his desk.
  3. Part 05 (`6:06–6:34`): 1 July 1858 Linnean reading has two empty chairs at the front; `"Wallace hadn't even been asked."`
  4. Part 05 (`6:54–7:03`): Murray's 22 Nov 1859 trade sale oversubscribed all ~1,250 copies of *On the Origin of Species* **before** the 24 Nov 1859 publication day.
- **One Explorer, on model** (round gold glasses, teal trenchcoat, finished crown), outside the first minute (`1:08` and `2:24`), never at a Short's frame 0.
- **Quality vs Fast:** use Quality (`Q`) for readable faces, candlelight/oil-lamp fall-off, water/fire, and wildlife. Use Fast (`F`) for simple tabletop objects, maps, and low-risk motion. Never use Fast on fragile light.
