**Script (60 words):**

Edmond Halley predicted that a comet would come back in seventeen fifty-eight. He used Isaac Newton's new law of gravity to work out its path. Halley died sixteen years before it was due. But on Christmas night, seventeen fifty-eight, a farmer looking through his telescope saw it appear, right on time. It has carried Halley's name ever since.

*Fact check:* Halley, *Synopsis* (1705); Halley died 1742; Palitzsch (a farmer and amateur astronomer near Dresden), 25 Dec 1758. The long's FACT_NOTES rows.
