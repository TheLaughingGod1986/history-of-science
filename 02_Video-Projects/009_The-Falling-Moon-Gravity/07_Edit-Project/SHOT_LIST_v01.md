## Rules for every plate

- **House look:** STUDIO_PLAYBOOK §5. Animistry-class 3D cartoon, moving from frame 0, never stills or Ken Burns. Silent picture; the VO is added in the edit.
- **No readable text in a plate.** The sums, 60 TIMES FURTHER, 3,600, 1 MINUTE = 1 SECOND, the *Principia* title page and every LABEL are **overlays in the edit** (Didot, house style). Pages and diagrams show shapes and lines, not legible words or numbers.
- **The apple never hits Newton's head.** It lands in the grass a few feet away (FACT_NOTES: a later embellishment).
- **Plague without horror.** Cambridge closing is shown as gates, carts and trunks. No sick people, no crosses on doors, no carts of bodies.
- **The Moon's fall is a curve, never a drop.** In every orbit plate the Moon or ball slides sideways and down along a smooth curve. Nothing ever plummets straight down at the Earth.
- **Space is real.** The ISS is shown as a generic station silhouette, with no readable agency logos or flags and no real astronaut likenesses.
- **One Explorer, on model** (round gold glasses, teal long coat, finished hair), at most once per part, and never at a Short's frame 0.
- **Quality vs Fast:** use Quality for readable faces, the Moon and night skies, the comet, glows, water and space. Fast is for diagrams and simple low-risk motion. Never use Fast on fragile light.
