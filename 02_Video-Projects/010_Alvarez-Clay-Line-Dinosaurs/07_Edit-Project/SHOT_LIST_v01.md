## Rules for every plate

- **House look:** STUDIO_PLAYBOOK §5. Animistry-class 3D cartoon, moving from frame 0, never stills or Ken Burns. Silent picture; the VO is added in the edit.
- **No readable text in a plate.** The graph's labels, 30×, the *Science* title, the map pins' names and every LABEL are **overlays in the edit** (Didot, house style). Pages, maps and screens show shapes and lines, not legible words or numbers.
- **No impact spectacle.** The asteroid strike is never shown hitting. No fireball, no burning animals, no dying dinosaurs. The catastrophe is shown through its evidence and its calm aftermath: dust dimming the Sun, a quiet forest, empty rock.
- **Real scientists, kind likenesses.** Walter and Luis Alvarez, Frank Asaro and Helen Michel are drawn in the house cartoon style, matched to period photos for age, hair and glasses (P1–P3), never caricatured.
- **Dinosaurs and birds:** the skeleton over the robin is a generic small feathered dinosaur, not a named species. No dinosaur is ever shown suffering.
- **One Explorer, on model** (round gold glasses, teal long coat, finished hair), at most once per part, and never at a Short's frame 0.
- **Quality vs Fast:** use Quality for readable faces, sunlit rock textures, glows, space dust and animals. Fast is for maps, diagrams and simple low-risk motion. Never use Fast on fragile light.
