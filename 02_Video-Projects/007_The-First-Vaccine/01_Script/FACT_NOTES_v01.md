## Script rules (lock before drafting)

- **Boy's test:** one calm factual line only (Phipps later received smallpox matter and did not take the disease). No dramatised fear, no close-up of cutting, no "experiment on a child" framing that invites outrage.
- **No gore:** inherit 005 Harvey red rules — no blood pools, no open wounds as spectacle, no pustule close-ups as the hero shot. Cowpox on a hand can be a teach card / soft mark, not horror.
- **No present-day vaccine politics:** this film ends in 1798 (Inquiry) and the after-act (1980 eradication + *vacca* etymology). No COVID, mandates, modern controversy, or "should you…" beats.
- **After-act:** WHO declaration that smallpox was eradicated (1980) + the word *vaccine* from Latin *vacca* (cow). That is the landing, not a policy lecture.
- **"First" needs care:** Benjamin Jesty (Dorset farmer) gave his family cowpox in **1774**, 22 years before Jenner. Farmers already believed cowpox protected you; **variolation** (deliberate smallpox inoculation) was common long before 1796. Jenner's step was the **test** and the **publication**, which turned a dairy rumour into medicine that spread. Script must say this in **one line** — also the lane's "proof" beat.
- **Blossom naming rule:** Blossom is **not** in the *Inquiry*. The name comes from later tradition; hide at St George's, London. Narrate only as "the cow, later called Blossom", or leave the name out. **Never** put Blossom on screen as a primary fact.
