## Rules for every plate

- **House look:** STUDIO_PLAYBOOK §5. Animistry-class 3D cartoon, moving from frame 0, never stills or Ken Burns. Silent picture; the VO is added in the edit.
- **No readable text in a plate.** VACCA → VACCINE, case-book dates, NO DISEASE FOLLOWED, the *Inquiry* title page, the 1980 headline and the board behind Pasteur are all **overlays in the edit** (Didot, house style). The plate shows the page, book or board with no legible words.
- **No gore.** Cowpox and smallpox are never shown as sores. A healed hand has soft round marks at most. The lancet scenes show the gesture, with no wound and no blood.
- **No plague imagery.** No crosses on doors, no carts of bodies. A smallpox street is shuttered and quiet.
- **One Explorer, on model** (round gold glasses, teal long coat, finished hair), at most once per part, and never at a Short's frame 0.
- **Quality vs Fast:** Quality for readable faces, crowds, glows and candle/lamp light; Fast for simple low-risk motion. Never Fast on fragile light.
