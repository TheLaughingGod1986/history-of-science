## Rules for every plate

- House look: premium 3D cartoon, moving from frame 0, never a still push or Ken Burns. Silent picture.
- No readable text in a Veo plate. Every `LABEL`, `WHO`, `WHEN`, `DATA`, `SCALE` and `FACT` is added in the edit. One label or card at a time. Never two code graphics back to back.
- Every named person gets a `WHO` card at first mention, with a credited archive portrait cut-out where one is free to use.
- Nothing graphic: no blood, no cutting, no instruments in use on a patient, no distressed faces held. The wince in Part 03 row 06 is one brief reaction.
- The Explorer appears twice, both after the first minute (1:12 and 4:15).
- Quality for readable faces, candlelight, water and crowds. Fast only for daylight objects, rooms and exteriors.
