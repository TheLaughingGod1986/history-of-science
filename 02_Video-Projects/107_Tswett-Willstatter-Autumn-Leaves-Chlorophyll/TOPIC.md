## 2. The film

- **People and dates (all to be sourced in SOURCES.md before any script lock; written from memory here, unverified):** In 1817 Pierre-Joseph Pelletier and Joseph Bienaimé Caventou named the green substance in leaves chlorophyll. In 1903 Mikhail Tswett, in Warsaw, described washing a leaf extract down a column of powdered chalk: it separated into green and yellow bands, and in 1906 he named the method chromatography ("colour writing"). Richard Willstätter won the 1915 Nobel Prize in Chemistry for work on chlorophyll and plant pigments.
- **Twist:** the yellow and orange are in the leaf all year and only show when the green is broken down in autumn; the reds are made new. Tswett's method was largely ignored for about 25 years, he died in 1919, and it later became one of chemistry's most used tools.
