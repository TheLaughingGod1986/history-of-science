## 2. Mandatory Primary Sources Required Before Script Lock (`facts` Lane)

Before any script is locked or any VO/picture spend is queued (once `HOS:107` enters `WIP.json["active"]`):
1. **Pierre-Joseph Pelletier & Joseph Bienaimé Caventou (`1817`, *Journal de Pharmacie et des Sciences Accessoires* vol. 3, pp. 486–491 / *Annales de Chimie et de Physique* ser. 2, vol. 9, `1818`, pp. 194–196):** *"Notice sur la matière verte des feuilles"* — exact quote on isolating and naming *chlorophylle* from green leaves.
2. **Mikhail Semyonovich Tswett (`21 March 1903` lecture to the Warsaw Society of Naturalists, *Trudy Varshavskogo Obshchestva Estestvoispytatelei, Otdelenie Biologii* vol. 14, `1905`, pp. 1–20; and `1906` *Berichte der Deutschen Botanischen Gesellschaft* vol. 24, pp. 316–323 *"Physikalisch-chemische Studien über das Chlorophyll. Die Adsorptionen"* & pp. 384–393 *"Adsorptionsanalyse und chromatographische Methode. Anwendung auf die Chemie des Chlorophylls"*):** Exact quotes on separating leaf pigments (`chlorophyll a`, `chlorophyll b`, `xanthophylls`, `carotenes`) on a calcium carbonate (`CaCO3`, chalk) column and coining *chromatography*.
3. **Richard Willstätter & Arthur Stoll (`1913`, *Untersuchungen über Chlorophyll: Methoden und Ergebnisse*, Julius Springer, Berlin; and Willstätter's `1915` Nobel Prize in Chemistry / `1920` Nobel Lecture *"On Plant Pigments"*):** Exact quotes on proving magnesium (`Mg`) is the central atom of chlorophyll (`C55H72O5N4Mg` for chlorophyll $a$), isolating pure carotenoids (`carotene` `C40H56`, `xanthophyll` `C40H56O2`), and why Tswett's column method was neglected until Richard Kuhn, Alfred Winterstein & Edgar Lederer (`1931`, *Hoppe-Seyler's Zeitschrift für physiologische Chemie* vol. 197, pp. 141–160).
4. **Plant physiology primary source (e.g., Philippe Matile `2000`, *Experimental Gerontology* / William A. Hoch, Eric L. Zeldin & Brent H. McCown `2001`, *Tree Physiology* / Lee et al. `2003`):** Exact quote verifying that yellow/orange carotenoids are present in the chloroplast thylakoid membranes throughout summer and unmasked as chlorophyll catabolism proceeds in autumn, whereas red/purple anthocyanins are newly synthesised in autumn senescing leaves.
