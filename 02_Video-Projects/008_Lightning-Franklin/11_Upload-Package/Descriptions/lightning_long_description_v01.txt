Why did Benjamin Franklin fly a kite into a lightning storm, on purpose?
In 1752 a kite, an iron key and a glass jar proved that lightning is electricity, and gave us the lightning rod.

For thousands of years, lightning set roofs on fire and church steeples were struck again and again. Franklin noticed that the spark from a Leyden jar looked, sounded and smelled like a tiny thunderstorm. So he wrote a list of twelve likenesses, and five famous words: let the experiment be made.
