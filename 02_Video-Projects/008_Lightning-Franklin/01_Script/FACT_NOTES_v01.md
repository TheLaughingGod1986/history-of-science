## Script rules (still locked)

- **No Hollywood electrocution spectacle.** Key / knuckle spark is the proof beat; stand under cover; silk ribbon stays dry — follow Franklin's own safety framing.
- **Priority care:** French electricians (**Dalibard / Delor, May 1752**) succeeded with the grounded rod before Franklin's kite (**~June 1752**). Script must not claim he was first in the world to draw cloud electricity — he proposed the test and proved it with a kite in Philadelphia.
- **Kite never struck:** charge from the cloud down the wet string, not a bolt through the kite.
- **Son age:** William Franklin assisting was an adult (~21), not a small child (Currier & Ives error).
- **Danger beat:** Georg Richmann killed 1753 — keep as caution after the spark, not spectacle.
