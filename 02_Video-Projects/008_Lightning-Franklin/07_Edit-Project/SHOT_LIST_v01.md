## Rules for every plate

- **House look:** STUDIO_PLAYBOOK §5. Animistry-class 3D cartoon, moving from frame 0, never stills or Ken Burns. Silent picture; the VO is added in the edit.
- **No readable text in a plate.** The 12-item list, LET THE EXPERIMENT BE MADE, the *Gazette* column, the *Poor Richard* page and every LABEL are **overlays in the edit** (Didot, house style). The plate shows the page or notebook with no legible words.
- **No electrocution spectacle** (FACT_NOTES script rules). Sparks are small and blue, from a key, a jar or a knuckle. Nobody is struck. Richmann's death is never shown: the room and the apparatus only, then a flash and a cut.
- **The kite is never struck.** No bolt ever touches the kite or the string. Charge is shown as a soft glow or fibres lifting, never as a lightning bolt down the string.
- **William is a young man of about twenty-one**, never a child (the Currier & Ives error). Franklin is in his mid-forties in 1749–52.
- **Lightning and fire:** a struck steeple can catch fire. Nobody burns, and no injured people are shown.
- **One Explorer, on model** (round gold glasses, teal long coat, finished hair), at most once per part, and never at a Short's frame 0.
- **Quality vs Fast:** use Quality for readable faces, crowds, lightning, sparks, glows and candlelight. Fast is for simple low-risk motion. Never use Fast on fragile light, which is most of this film, so the Quality share is higher than 007's.
