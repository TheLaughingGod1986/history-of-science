## 1. The Discovery Story (Person, Date & Twist — UNVERIFIED)

- **Familiar phenomenon:** Every hair on your head grows out of its follicle as crystal-clear, colourless keratin that has to be painted from the inside before it emerges—and from a 2005 stem-cell discovery to a January 2020 Harvard nerve-stress experiment and an April 2023 NYU 3D follicle study, scientists uncovered why extreme stress drains a hair root's pigment supply in days and why aging hair turns grey when its pigment stem cells get physically trapped inside the follicle bulge.
- **Person, place & date:**
  - **Louis-Nicolas Vauquelin** (Paris, **1806**, *Annales de Chimie* `58: 41–53`, `"Expériences sur les cheveux"`) & **Thomas B. Fitzpatrick, Aaron B. Lerner & Makoto Seiji** (**1950–1965**, *Science* `112: 223–225`; *Ann. N.Y. Acad. Sci.* `100: 497–533`): Vauquelin boils black, red, and white hair to prove every hair shaft is made of the same colourless structural keratin matrix tinted by pigment granules; Fitzpatrick, Lerner, and Seiji map how hair-bulb **melanocytes** use **tyrosinase** inside **melanosomes** to inject `eumelanin` and `pheomelanin` into the growing hair shaft during **anagen**.
  - **Emi K. Nishimura, Scott A. Granter & David E. Fisher** (Harvard Medical School / Dana-Farber, **April 2002**, *Nature* `416: 854–860`; **4 February 2005**, *Science* `307(5710): 720–724`, `"Mechanisms of hair graying: incomplete melanocyte stem cell maintenance in the niche"`): discover the dormant reservoir of **melanocyte stem cells (`McSCs`)** in the hair follicle's **bulge and sub-bulge niche** that rebuilds the pigment factory at each new hair cycle, and prove that age-related greying begins when this stem-cell vault fails to self-renew.
  - **Bing Zhang, Sai Ma, Inês Rachmin, et al. & Ya-Chieh Hsu** (Harvard University, **22 January 2020**, *Nature* `577(7792): 676–681`, `"Hyperactivation of sympathetic nerves drives depletion of melanocyte stem cells"`) & **Ayelet M. Rosenberg, et al. & Martin Picard** (Columbia University, **22 June 2021**, *eLife* `10: e67437`): Zhang & Hsu prove neither cortisol nor immune attack causes stress greying—instead, **sympathetic fight-or-flight nerve fibres** wrapped around the hair bulge dump **noradrenaline** onto `ADRB2` receptors on dormant melanocyte stem cells, forcing **100% of the stem-cell vault** to fire, differentiate, and empty out within **days**; Picard & Rosenberg measure individual human hairs with white-to-dark bands showing pigment recovery when psychological stress drops.
  - **Qi Sun, Wendy Lee, Hai Hu, et al. & Mayumi Ito** (NYU Grossman School of Medicine, **19 April 2023**, *Nature* `617(7962): 778–785`, `"Dedifferentiation maintains melanocyte stem cells in a dynamic niche"`): use 2-year longitudinal **3D intravital two-photon imaging** to discover that melanocyte stem cells must reversibly de-differentiate as they shuttle between the **`WNT`-rich hair germ** and the **`WNT`-poor bulge-sub-bulge**—and that after repeated shedding cycles, over **50% of the stem cells get physically stranded** in the bulge-sub-bulge compartment, unable to reach the `WNT` signal to make colour.
