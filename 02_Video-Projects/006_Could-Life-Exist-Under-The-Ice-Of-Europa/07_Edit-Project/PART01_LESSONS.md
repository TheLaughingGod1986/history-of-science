## Locked preferences (user QA)

1. Crack skim + Omni SFX while VO says scars = **keep / match forever**.
2. No last-frame freezes.
3. No dual Orbit.
4. Lean into **diving into Europa’s ocean** when narration goes under the ice.
5. Soft soundtrack transitions between Omni plates.
6. **Character in the scene** (user Flow share): turns, propulsion/wake, flies with intent + SFX — Orbit *does* things in the world, not a floating sticker. Carry into Part 02+ prompts/QA.
7. **Underwater propulsion lock:** soft underside glow (space-like) throughout — no mid-part switch to flame plumes in water.
8. **Under-ice / ocean minutes:** add light continuous water-movement / bubble bed under Omni SFX so motion through water reads in the mix.

## Inventory recipe (~60s VO)

~8 unique Omni plates × ~8s @ 1× → cover VO without pad.  
Part 01 locked plates 01–06 from v02; 07–08 from v03 dive set.

## Do not repeat

- Equal-slice VO windows on 3 clips + `tpad` clone.
- Assembling before enough unique motion exists.
- Hard `concat` copy of Omni audio across scene cuts.
- Shipping a plate without frame-spotting for a second Orbit.
