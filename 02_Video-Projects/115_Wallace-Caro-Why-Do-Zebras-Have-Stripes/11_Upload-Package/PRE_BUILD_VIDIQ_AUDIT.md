## 1. The Discovery Story (Person, Date & Twist — UNVERIFIED)

- **Familiar phenomenon:** Why zebras have bold black-and-white stripes across the open African savannah.
- **Person, place & date:**
  - **Charles Darwin & Alfred Russel Wallace** (**1871–1889**, *The Descent of Man* & *Darwinism*): debate why zebras evolved high-contrast stripes, launching 140 years of competing hypotheses (predator camouflage in tall grass, motion dazzle, social recognition, convective air cooling, and biting-fly deterrence).
  - **Gábor Horváth & Susanne Åkesson** (Hungary / Lund, **February 2012**, *Journal of Experimental Biology*): demonstrate using sticky oil-coated trays and horse models that blood-seeking tabanid horseflies are guided by horizontally polarised light and strongly avoid narrow black-and-white stripes.
  - **Tim Caro & Martin How** (UC Davis / University of Bristol, **2014–2019**, *Nature Communications* & *PLOS ONE*): map all 7 wild equid species across Africa (`2014`, proving striping tracks biting-fly abundance rather than lions or temperature), prove lions cannot resolve stripes beyond `50 m` in daylight (`2016`), and dress domestic English horses in **zebra-striped cloth coats** (`2019`, Somerset, UK) to film horseflies failing to decelerate and bouncing right off the stripes (`cutting landing rates by >75%`).
