## 2. Mandatory Primary Sources Required Before Script Lock (`facts` Lane)

Before any script is locked or any VO/picture spend is queued (once `HOS:106` enters `WIP.json["active"]`):
1. **Aristotle (*Historia Animalium*, Book VI, ch. 16):** Exact quote on eels having neither eggs nor milt and arising from the "entrails of the earth" (*gēs enteron*) / mud.
2. **Sigmund Freud (`1877`, *Sitzungsberichte der Kaiserlichen Akademie der Wissenschaften, Mathematisch-Naturwissenschaftliche Classe*, Wien, Bd. 75, I. Abth., pp. 419–431):** *"Beobachtungen über Gestaltung und feineren Bau der als Hoden beschriebenen Lappenorgane des Aals"* — exact quote on dissecting ~400 eels at Carl Claus's zoological station in Trieste (`1876`) searching for Syrski's organ.
3. **Giovanni Battista Grassi & Salvatore Calandruccio (`1896` / `1897`, *Proceedings of the Royal Society of London*, vol. 60, pp. 260–271 / *Rendiconti della R. Accademia dei Lincei*):** Exact quote proving *Leptocephalus brevirostris* is the larval stage of *Anguilla vulgaris* (*Anguilla anguilla*).
4. **Johannes Schmidt (`1922` / `1923`, *Philosophical Transactions of the Royal Society of London B*, vol. 211, pp. 179–208 / *Nature* 1922):** *"The Breeding Places of the Eel"* — exact quote and larval-length contour data (`<10 mm` leptocephali in the Sargasso Sea).
5. **Rosalind M. Wright et al. (`13 October 2022`, *Scientific Reports* 12, 15362, `https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-022-19693-1`):** *"First direct evidence of adult European eels migrating to their breeding place in the Sargasso Sea"* — exact quote on pop-up satellite archival tags tracking adult *Anguilla anguilla* to the Sargasso Sea.
