## 2. Topic Brief — `HOS:101` (*How Does Life Survive at the Bottom of the Ocean?*)

### 2.1 Core Packaging
- **Title A (Main — Question):** `How Does Life Survive at the Bottom of the Ocean?` *(49 chars)*
- **Title B (Test & Compare — Statement):** `Deep Sea Vents: The World That Lives Without Sun` *(48 chars)*
- **Title C (Discovery / Experiment Angle):** `The 1977 Dive That Found Life Without Sunlight` *(46 chars)*
- **One-Line Hook:** On 17 February `1977`, three geologists diving `2.5` kilometres into the pitch-black Pacific in the tiny submarine *Alvin* expected a dead volcanic crack—and instead switched on their headlights to find a lush forest of `2-metre` crimson tube worms living without a single ray of sunlight.
- **Thumbnail Concept:** Deep-sea 3D house-style frame: the tiny white sphere of submersible *Alvin*'s headlight beam cutting through pitch-black water onto a towering black-smoker chimney (`400 °C` mineral plume) surrounded by bright crimson-plumed giant tube worms (`Riftia pachyptila`), with a 3-word Didot cover label `LIFE WITHOUT SUN` (or `400°C / LIFE`). High-contrast crimson/amber glow against abyssal black (`168×94` mobile legibility PASS, `LEARNINGS.md` Rule 4).

### 2.2 Person, Date & Twist (Historical Discovery Chain + Teach-on-Screen Plan)
1. **Edward Forbes (`1843`, Aegean Sea — The "Azoic Hypothesis"):** British naturalist Edward Forbes dredges the Aegean Sea and declares that below `300 fathoms` (`~550 metres`), in total darkness and crushing cold, zero life can exist—because every food chain on Earth supposedly begins with sunlight (`WHO: Edward Forbes (1815–1854) — Marine naturalist`, `WHEN: Aegean Sea, 1843`, `DATA: Depth chart — Sunlight zone 200 m vs Forbes 550 m limit vs Galápagos Rift 2,500 m · SCALE: Deeper than six Empire State Buildings stacked`).
