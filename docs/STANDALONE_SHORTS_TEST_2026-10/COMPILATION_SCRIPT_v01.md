For most of history, the things that run your life were invisible.

The blood moving round your body. The air a tree is built from. The germs on your hands. The atoms inside everything.

Nobody could see them. So people had to prove them. With a band round an arm. A willow in a pot. A flask with a curved neck. A table with empty gaps. A glowing screen. And a sheet of gold.
