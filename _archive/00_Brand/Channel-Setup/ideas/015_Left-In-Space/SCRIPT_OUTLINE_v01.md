# 015 — Script outline v01 (Left in space)

**Title (96):** The 7 Strangest Things We've Accidentally Left in Space | History of Science  
**Runtime target:** 12–18 min  
**Patterns:** object-by-object journey · emotional anchoring · wonder ending (intentional Golden Record)

## Promise

> Humans are messy explorers. We've dropped tools, launched cars, and littered orbits — but one object was meant to leave. Seven things. Six accidents. One love letter to the dark.

## Structure

### Object stops (draft roster — verify facts before script lock)
1. Spacewalk tool drop (spatula / pliers lore — confirm which mission)
2. A glove or camera lost on EVA
3. Tesla Roadster / Starman
4. A piece of station or shuttle history with a human story
5. A probe component / “message” adjacent oddity
6. Orbital debris with a surprising origin story
7. **Voyager Golden Record** — the intentional closer

Each beat: object → how it got there → what it reveals about us.

### Closing
- Orbit with the Record motif: we left a voice on purpose
- Wonder CTA — soft, human, not preachy

## Tone

Palate cleanser after heavy mystery weeks. Keep science honest (debris ≠ cute myth). No conspiracy.
