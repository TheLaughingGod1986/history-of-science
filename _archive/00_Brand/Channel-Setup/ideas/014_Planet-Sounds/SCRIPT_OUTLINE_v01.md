# 014 — Script outline v01 (Planet sounds)

**Title (98):** Every Planet Has a Sound — and What They Recorded Is Stranger Than Anyone Expected | History of Science  
**Runtime target:** 15–20 min  
**Patterns:** assumption-flip · planet-by-planet tour · Orbit radio guide · wonder ending

## Promise

> Space isn't the silent void most of us picture. Spacecraft have recorded planetary radio voices — and Orbit is going to tune the dial, one world at a time, until we hear the one voice that might be the strangest of all: ours.

## Structure

### 0. Cold open
- Orbit at a cosmic radio console
- Body anchor: “Press your palm to the speaker. What you're about to hear started as invisible storms around other worlds.”
- Honesty beat: these are radio emissions translated into sound — still real data, still awe

### 1. Tour stops (one wonder each)
Suggested order (adjust to cleared audio):
1. **Jupiter** — whistlers / chorus
2. **Saturn** — auroral radio song
3. **Earth's neighborhood contrast** — Moon / barren cues
4. **Mars** — thin silence vs plasma whispers
5. **Optional ice giants** if audio + runtime allow

Each stop: 1 visual motif + 1 science sentence + Orbit reaction.

### 2. Mid-film science beat
- How plasma / magnetospheres sing
- Why vacuum still has “voices” at radio wavelengths
- Keep poetic, not textbook

### 3. Closing reveal
- Earth's electromagnetic song — lightning, aurora, human RF era
- What makes it unique in this sample
- Orbit listens outward: soft wonder CTA
