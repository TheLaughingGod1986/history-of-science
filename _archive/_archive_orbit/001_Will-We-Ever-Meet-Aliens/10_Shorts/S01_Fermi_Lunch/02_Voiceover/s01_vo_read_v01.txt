If alien civilisations are common… where is everybody?

That question has a name: the Fermi Paradox.
Physicist Enrico Fermi supposedly asked a version of it over lunch.
The maths can sound optimistic — hundreds of billions of stars, countless planets.
But the sky stays quiet.
No radio greetings. No city lights on other worlds. No obvious megastructures.
Some say life is rare. Some say technology doesn’t last. Some say… they’re hiding.
And some say we’re simply early to the party.

So is the silence empty — or unfinished?
