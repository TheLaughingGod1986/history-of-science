In nineteen seventy-seven, a radio telescope caught something strange.

A narrow-band signal — strong, brief, and hard to explain as ordinary noise.
Astronomer Jerry Ehman circled the printout and wrote one word: Wow!
It became the most famous maybe-in-the-sky moment in SETI history.
But here’s the catch.
It never cleanly repeated.
No confirmed follow-up. No second hello.
So was it a glitch? A rare natural burst? Or a message that passed us once… and kept going?

One scream. Then silence.
