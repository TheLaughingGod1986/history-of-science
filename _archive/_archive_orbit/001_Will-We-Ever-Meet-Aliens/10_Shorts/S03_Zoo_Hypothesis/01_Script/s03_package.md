## 3. Full script (~120 words · ~47s)

**[0–3s HOOK]**  
What if aliens already know we’re here… and they’re choosing not to visit?

**[3–40s]**  
That’s the zoo hypothesis.  
The idea that advanced civilisations might watch younger ones without interfering — like a cosmic nature reserve with a quiet rule: do not disturb.  
It wouldn’t mean flying saucers over cities.  
It would mean silence on purpose.  
Maybe they think contact would change us too fast.  
Maybe they wait until a species is ready.  
Or maybe the “zoo” idea is just us telling a comforting story about why the sky won’t talk.

**[40–50s END]**  
Protection… or imagination?  
I’m Orbit. We weigh the hopeful and unsettling answers in the full film.
