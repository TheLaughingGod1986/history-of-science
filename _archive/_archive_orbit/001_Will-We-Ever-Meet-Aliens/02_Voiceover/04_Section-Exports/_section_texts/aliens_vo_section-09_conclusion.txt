So — will we ever meet aliens?

Maybe as a signal.
Maybe as a biosignature.
Maybe never as a conversation.

But the search still matters. Because looking for life elsewhere is also a way of understanding life here — how rare it might be… how fragile it might be… and how precious a noisy little planet becomes when the rest of the sky stays quiet.

I’m Orbit. If you want more big questions about a bigger universe — stick around. Please like and subscribe… it helps us keep exploring.

And next time you look up… remember: the silence isn’t empty.

It’s an invitation to keep listening.

Until then, Orbit’s advice is simple.

Stay curious. Stay careful. Don’t confuse silence with an answer — and don’t confuse a single weird signal with a civilisation.

The universe has given us a mystery big enough to last generations.

The honest work… is to keep asking it better.
