Look up on a clear night… and the sky feels crowded.

Thousands of stars. Billions more beyond them. Whole galaxies, spinning quietly in the dark.

So… where is everybody?

If the universe is so big, and so old — why hasn’t anyone waved back?

I’m Orbit. And tonight, we’re sitting with one of the biggest questions humans keep returning to:

Will we ever meet aliens?

Not in a movie. Not as a meme. As a real question — about biology, distance, time… and what “meeting” even means.
