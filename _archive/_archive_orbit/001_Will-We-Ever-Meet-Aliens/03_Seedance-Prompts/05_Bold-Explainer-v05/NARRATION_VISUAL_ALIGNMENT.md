# Narration-to-Visual Alignment — Bold Explainer v05

## Editorial contract

- The narration is the master timeline.
- Every board starts and ends with the matching narration passage below.
- Each board contains four unique panels; no panel or video source may appear
  twice.
- The opening question plays before any branding.
- Insert the approved Orbit brand card for a two-second hold immediately after
  “So why has nobody answered?”
- Resume immediately on “This is Orbit.”
- Information cards are reserved for the small number of facts listed below.
  They must not appear every few seconds.
- Orbit is a separate animated overlay. Keep him on the lower-right stage unless
  a subject occupies that area; do not jump corners between adjacent scenes.

## Information-card restraint

Only the twelve cards explicitly named above are allowed in the first review
master. Each should:

- remain visible for four to six seconds;
- use one short heading and, at most, one supporting line;
- appear no more often than roughly once per minute;
- never obscure Orbit, the key scientific subject, or captions;
- enter and leave with a quiet 200–300 ms fade.

## Orbit presence target

- Aim for 38–42% of the final runtime.
- Prefer continuous 10–18 second performances over one- or two-second flashes.
- Reuse the approved rig, but vary the performance by pose, entrance time,
  eye direction, hover phase, and exit.
- Never use the same Orbit pose in two consecutive appearances.
- Orbit should first look at the visual subject, then occasionally acknowledge
  the audience.

## Validation

Before export, verify:

1. All 96 scene IDs occur exactly once.
2. The source hash for every scene is unique.
3. Each board’s narration range matches its four panels.
4. The brand card begins after the hook and holds for two seconds.
5. No card appears outside the approved list.
6. Orbit does not change corners without a visible travel transition.
7. The final line lands on the archive-pattern visual, not generic space B-roll.
