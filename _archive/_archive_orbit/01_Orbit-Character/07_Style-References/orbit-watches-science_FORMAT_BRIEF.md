## The show idea in one line

Orbit sits in a soft futuristic room and **reacts** to science playing on a hologram / monitor — wonder, love, thinking, “wow” — while Ben narrates.

## Shot vocabulary (from the board)

| Beat | Camera / set | Orbit performance |
|------|----------------|-------------------|
| Hook | Medium, hologram screen + space footage | Star-eyes / hands-to-face excitement |
| Love | Similar, heart pop (optional, sparingly) | Wide cream eyes, soft hover |
| Think | Slightly wider, smaller hologram | Finger-to-chin / head tilt (stubby arms only) |
| Desk | Physical monitor + notebook props | Speech-bubble moment OR VO only (prefer VO) |

## Visual rules

**Steal**
- Premium Pixar-like CG; shallow DOF; warm Orbit vs cool blue UI
- Clean grey futuristic interior (not deep-space void every shot)
- Science content lives *on the screen* (cells, volcanoes, planets, molecules)
- Expression-led storytelling

**Orbit lock (non-negotiable)**
- Canonical body: rounded orange, black faceplate, **cream eyes**, single antenna, **stubby arms**
- No claw/finger redesign in masters (board shows claws — treat as mood only)
- No LED pixel faces; no etched cartoon mouths as default
- No random UI gibberish; English only if text is needed
- Prefer Ben VO over on-screen speech bubbles for long-form
